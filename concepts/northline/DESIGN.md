# Design System: Northline Dental

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Two Answers Before the Scroll"**

Northline Dental is a one-page dental-practice site that answers the two questions a nervous patient has before they scroll ("can I get in soon" and "do you take my insurance") and spends the rest of the page taking the dread apart: prices for the common visits posted, a written estimate promised before any treatment, forms done on the phone from the couch, comfort options named, a raised hand that stops everything. The first screen carries the promise, a live status line that says the practice is open and until when the phones are answered, three real openings computed from the schedule, the six plans it is in-network with, and one teal "Find a time". The world is a light room under mist: mist ground, deep teal-navy ink, and one teal worn by the action pill, the dot on every tile and chip, and the status line. Remove the copy and you would still recognize it: mist ground, one teal pill, three white tiles with a teal dot, hyperlegible type at a generous size.

It is VINCERE's fifth template base ("one structure, many skins") and the sibling of Sasso, Apex, Kestrel, and Auburn. Every color on the page lives in one token block at the top of `northline.css`; a skin is a second, complete token block on `html[data-skin]` and nothing structural changes beneath it. Two alternate skins ship (Slate, Warm) and match the three-way switcher in the VINCERE home page's concept panel. Like Kestrel and Auburn, the accent is split into a fill (`--accent`) and what teal wears as small running text (`--accent-text`), deeper on the two light skins so it clears contrast on the ground and identical on Slate. Northline adds a second pair the siblings lack: `--tile` (a white wash at 72% over the mist) and `--tile-dot` (a slightly brighter teal) for the availability tiles and the insurance chips. The `--veil` and `--chrome` triplets equal the ground so the hero veil and the nav dissolve into the page. Northline is fictional, carries `noindex, nofollow`, and is never listed in the sitemap; its palette and type are deliberately not VINCERE tokens (see the root DESIGN.md's "Concept Previews" exception).

The type is the accessibility argument made visible: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 1.125rem for body and never below 0.8125rem anywhere, ledes up to 1.3rem, weights held to 400, 500, and 700. Density is generous: sections breathe on a clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem) rhythm (the top of the sibling range), copy is capped at 40 to 62ch, and prices sit on hairline rows rather than in cards. Only four things are raised surfaces (the tiles, the insurance card, the hours card, the fields), and only the fixed mobile bar carries a shadow. Confirmed rejections: a stock smile hero, "we accept most insurance" with no list, a phone number as the only next step, a PDF of forms to print, reviews, ratings, "voted best", photographs of mouths or instruments, and a tracked-caps eyebrow above any headline.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three mist steps (#EFF6F8, #E3EEF3, #FFFFFF) do the sectioning; hairlines at 16% and 32% ink do the ruling
- One teal in two roles: #17798A as fill (the pill, the status dot, selection, the caret, the status-box wash and focus halo) and #146A79 as small text (the status line, "Text us the card", today's hours row, link hover, the focus ring); Slate uses one value for both
- A tile pair beside the accent: white at 72% over the mist (`--tile`) for tiles and chips, with a 9px or 8px dot in a brighter teal (`--tile-dot`, #1B8A9C)
- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 700 at -0.02 to -0.032em for the H1, every H2, group titles, step and card titles, the tile time, and the wordmark; 400 to 500 for everything read, at 1.125rem base; nothing smaller than 0.8125rem
- Two radii: pills (999px) on every action and chip, with the nav pill filled with ink; 12px on tiles, cards, photos, fields, and the status box; circles only as 8px, 9px, and 10px dots
- Flat: no surface, button, card, tile, or field is lifted; the one shadow is under the fixed mobile bar
- Tabular numerals on prices, the tile times, the status line, the hours table, and the footer hours
- A skin is a token block on `html[data-skin]`, selected by `?skin=<id>`, with the accent split into fill and text, the tile pair redefined, and the veil equal to the ground
- One easing (out-expo) and three durations (160/320/700ms); the hero stack rises in an 80ms stagger, the hero photograph drifts 4% over 12s, and nothing else on the page animates on scroll

## Colors

A cool, low-chroma palette where three mist steps carry structure, teal-navy ink carries every word and number, one teal carries the action a nervous patient is looking for, and white tiles at 72% over the mist carry the three openings.

### Primary
- **Teal** (`{colors.accent}`): the fill accent. Fills "Find a time" in the hero, the form submit ("Request this time"), the insurance card's "Text a photo of your card", "Start your forms", and the mobile bar; is the 10px dot before the status line when open; is `accent-color`, `caret-color`, and text selection; turns a tile's border on hover and a field's border on focus; borders the sent-form status box and washes it (8%) and the field focus halo (22%) through the `--accent-rgb` triplet. Not used as text on the light skins.
- **Teal Text** (`{colors.accent-text}`): what teal wears as small running text on the ground: the live status line, the "Text us the card." link in the insurance strip, today's row in the hours table, a nav link or text link on hover. Marked in the build at 5.7:1 on the mist ground. Split from the fill so a light skin can deepen the text without darkening the pill; Slate sets both to the same aqua. Also the hover fill of the accent pill.
- **Teal Ink** (`{colors.accent-ink}`): text on the teal fill, white, marked 5.1:1 on the teal.
- **Teal Hover** (`{colors.accent-hover}`): the deeper teal the accent pill turns on hover, with a 1px rise. Equal to Teal Text on Sky and Warm.
- **Focus** (`{colors.focus}`): the global 3px focus outline at 3px offset, cut at 12px. Teal Text on the light skins; the aqua on Slate.

### Secondary
- **Tile** (`{colors.tile}`): white at 72% over the mist. The ground of the three availability tiles and of every insurance chip (hero strip and insurance section). A wash, not a solid, so the tile sits on the hero photograph and on the band alike. 7% white on Slate, 80% on Warm.
- **Tile Dot** (`{colors.tile-dot}`): a slightly brighter teal than the fill, used only as the 9px dot on a tile and the 8px dot on a chip. It reads as "open" without being the action. Equal to the accent on Slate; a lighter rust on Warm.

### Neutral
- **Mist** (`{colors.ground}`): page ground, the `theme-color`, and the source of the `--veil` and `--chrome` triplets (239, 246, 248) that build the hero veil, the nav, and the mobile bar; also the text on the ink-filled pill and skip link.
- **Mist Deep** (`{colors.ground-2}`): the alternating band (insurance, practice, book), ruled top and bottom with Line.
- **White** (`{colors.ground-3}`): raised surfaces: form fields, the insurance card, the hours card, the ground behind a loading photograph.
- **Ink** (`{colors.ink}`): headings, body, the wordmark, prices and row names, tile times, chips, field text, the nav pill's fill, the outline button's text and hover border, facts' terms, the status box's text, the "In-network:" label. A deep teal-navy, not a neutral grey.
- **Ink Soft** (`{colors.ink-soft}`): ledes, the hero sub line, nav links, service descriptions, step text, insurance card copy, hours values, facts' definitions, practice copy, the footer, and the field hover border. Marked 7.4:1 on Mist.
- **Ink Muted** (`{colors.ink-muted}`): tile day labels, the board caption, "Don't see yours?", notes, fine print, placeholders, the "Optional" mark, the row qualifier (`x-rays included`, `returning`), the select chevron, the demo note, the concept credit, and the status dot when closed. Marked 5.2:1 on Mist and 4.9:1 on Mist Deep.
- **Line** and **Line Strong** (`{colors.line}`, `{colors.line-strong}`): 16% and 32% ink. Line rules price and service rows, hours rows, band seams, tile, chip, and card borders, the stuck nav, the bar's top, the footer, and the footer's concept seam; Line Strong underlines group titles and text links, tops each new-patient step at 2px, borders every field, outline button, and bar button at rest, and colors the scrollbar thumb.
- **Error** (`{colors.error}`): a brick red for invalid field borders, error messages, and the status box's error border; salmon on Slate.

### Skins
A skin is a second token block on `html[data-skin="<id>"]` that redefines the same names. `?skin=slate` or `?skin=warm` on the URL sets the attribute from an inline script in `<head>`, before first paint, so the home page's "Open the full concept" link opens the look the visitor chose in the switcher. The veil and chrome triplets always equal the skin's ground.

- **Slate** (`{colors.slate-*}`): near-black blue-grey grounds, pale ink, and an aqua in place of the teal that is both fill and text (`slate-accent-text` 8.6:1 on the ground) with near-black on it; hover a paler aqua. Tiles become a 7% white wash with an aqua dot; hairlines stay at 16% and 32% of the pale ink; `color-scheme` flips to dark and the error softens to salmon. The only dark skin.
- **Warm** (`{colors.warm-*}`): cream grounds, warm near-black ink, and a rust in place of the teal: fill `warm-accent` with a white label (4.9:1), text `warm-accent-text` (5.2:1 on the ground), hover the same deeper rust. Tiles are an 80% white wash with a lighter rust dot. Light; `color-scheme` stays light.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Teal Rule.** Every use of the accent names which role it is: `--accent` on a fill with `--accent-ink` on it, `--accent-text` as small text on the ground. On a light skin the text value is deeper than the fill, so the same teal clears contrast in a word that it clears in a pill; a light skin that sets `color: var(--accent)` on running text is a bug. A dark skin may set both to one value.

**The Tile Pair Rule.** A tile or chip is `--tile` on a Line border with a `--tile-dot` circle before its text. The tile is a white wash, never a solid, so it sits on the photograph and on the band alike; the dot is the brighter teal, never the fill, so the pill stays the only action-colored thing on the screen. A skin redefines both.

**The Veil Is the Ground Rule.** `--veil` and `--chrome` are the ground as an RGB triplet, never a separate tint. The hero veil fades the photograph into the page, and the nav and mobile bar are the page at 65% and 92% opacity with blur. A skin that changes its ground changes all three.

**The Token Block Rule.** Every color on the page resolves to a token in section 1 of `northline.css`. A skin redefines that block and nothing else; the only skin-scoped selector below it is `color-scheme`. A brand that needs a structural override is a new base, not a skin.

**The Mist Hairline Rule.** Rules are 1px of ink at 16% (Line) or 32% (Line Strong), and 2px of Line Strong on top of a new-patient step; there are no solid divider colors and no border in the accent except a hovered tile, a focused field, and the sent-form status box. The dot does the pointing; the line does the ruling.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (with Atkinson Hyperlegible, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial)
**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (same stack)

One family, loaded from Google Fonts at 400, 500, 700 only.

**Character:** A face designed for low-vision readers, set larger than a dental site usually dares. At 700 with tight negative tracking it is plain and certain: the headline says what the practice does and stops. At 400 and 500 it does all the reading, the labeling, and the numbers, at 1.125rem so a patient reading on a phone in a parking lot does not have to zoom. The size is the accessibility point on the card, made as a type decision rather than a claim.

### Named Rules
**The Hyperlegible Size Rule.** Body is 1.125rem, ledes run to 1.3rem, and nothing read is smaller than 0.9375rem (the two Label uses at 0.8125rem are suffixes, not copy). Sizes are larger than a dental site's usual by design; do not tighten them to fit a layout. Weights are 400, 500, and 700; there is no 600 on the page.

**The Sentence-Case Signal Rule.** The hero's context sits below the H1 as a sentence-case status line in Teal Text, never above it as a tracked-caps eyebrow. A step leads with its title over a 2px rule, never with a numeral or a label; the `<ol>` carries the sequence.

**The Tabular Rule.** Anything with a digit that lines up (prices, tile times, the status line, buttons with a phone number, the hours table, the footer hours) sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Prices are one number in Ink 700 with no prefix; there is no "from" on this page because everything not priced is quoted in writing first.

## Layout

A single 1120px wrap (`min(100% - 2 * gutter, 1120px)`) with a fluid gutter (clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)) and a fluid section rhythm (clamp(5rem, 10vw, 9rem)), the most generous of the five bases. Bands alternate Mist (services, new patients, visit) and Mist Deep (insurance, practice, book) with a Line hairline at each seam. The nav is sticky at 76px on 65% chrome; `scroll-padding-top` is 84px. The hero pulls up under the nav (`margin-top: -76px`, `padding-top: 76px`), is `min(100svh, 940px)` tall, and aligns its content to the bottom, padded clamp(3rem, 8vw, 6rem) above and clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 5rem) below.

The hero stacks H1, status line, sub line, the actions row (teal pill and outline call button with an inline phone icon, wrapping at gap 0.75rem 1rem), the availability board (three tiles in a 3-column grid at gap 0.6rem, max 720px, with the caption spanning the row), and the insurance strip (label, six chips, and a "Don't see yours?" line that takes its own row), all bottom-left over the photograph. The photograph is positioned at 50% 45%; the veil is two gradients in the ground triplet, vertical 42% → 28% at 25% → 90% at 58% → 100% at the bottom, and horizontal 75% → 50% at 45% → 58% at the right edge. The veil was raised in the finish round (from a clearer right side) so the operatory chair reads as a pale shape and the window light, not the equipment, carries the right half.

Breakpoints are content-specific: 480px (below it the board stacks 1-up and each tile becomes a single baseline row with the dot, day, a " ·", and the time), 600px (below it price rows wrap and the qualifier drops to its own line; from it the steps go 2-up, form rows 1fr/1fr, and the form foot splits button and note auto/1fr), 760px (nav links appear), 800px (service groups 2-up), 900px (mobile bar hides and the body's bottom padding drops; services and new-patient heads go 1fr/1fr with the lede right-aligned at 40ch; insurance 1.15fr/0.85fr; practice 0.95fr/1.05fr; visit 1.1fr/0.9fr with the hours card sticky at 100px; book 0.8fr/1.2fr; footer 1.2fr/1fr with the concept line spanning both), 960px (nav "Book online" appears), and 1000px (steps 4-up). Below 900px the body reserves `64px + safe-area-inset-bottom` for the fixed action bar (Find a time / Call / Forms, 1.4fr/1fr/1fr).

Photography is two Unsplash placeholders of rooms, never of mouths or instruments: the hero operatory (`object-fit: cover`, with `srcset` at 900/1600/2400, `fetchpriority="high"`) and a 4:3 treatment-room frame in the practice section (600/1000/1400, lazy, cut at 12px). Grid children carry `min-width: 0` so an image's intrinsic width cannot blow out a column; price names ellipsize above 600px rather than wrap.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from the three mist steps (page, band, white surface), from the white tile wash sitting on the mist, and from ink hairlines; no button, card, tile, field, or nav carries a shadow. The hero photograph carries no shadow: it is under the page, not on it, and the veil does the blending. Nav and bar are translucent chrome (65% resting, 92% stuck or fixed) with `blur(14px)`. The one box-shadow on the page is a soft upward cast under the fixed mobile bar, so it separates from the content scrolling beneath it; it is chrome, not a surface. One zero-offset ring exists and is state, not depth: the field focus halo in teal.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Bar** (`box-shadow: 0 -10px 30px -18px rgba(16, 55, 63, 0.25)`): the fixed mobile action bar only. Hard-coded in Sky's ink; it does not follow the skin.
- **Field focus** (`0 0 0 4px rgba(var(--accent-rgb), 0.22)` with a Teal border): the only accent halo, and it is state, not elevation. `--accent-rgb` follows the skin.
- **Focus ring** (`outline: 3px solid var(--focus); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 12px`): the global keyboard ring in Teal Text (aqua on Slate). 3px, one heavier than the siblings, for the same reason the type is larger.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Room Rule.** Nothing on the page is lifted. Surfaces are flat at rest and flat on hover; hover is a 1px rise, a border change, a fill change, or a color change, never a shadow. The only shadow belongs to the fixed bar, and only because the bar is chrome over scrolling content.

**The Light Room Rule.** A photograph shows a room with a window, in color, under the mist veil; never a mouth, an instrument, or a stock smile. The hero veil is the ground and lands solid at the bottom under the copy; the practice photograph is clipped at 12px and carries no border and no shadow.

## Shapes

Two radii and a circle. Every action and chip is a pill (999px): the teal "Find a time" in the hero, the form, and the bar; the ink-filled "Book online" in the nav; the outline "Call (218) 555-0163"; the bar's Call and Forms; the skip link; every insurance chip. Every surface and field is cut at 12px (`--radius`): the three tiles, the insurance card, the hours card, the practice photograph, inputs and selects, the status box, and the focus outline. Circles appear only as dots: 8px before a chip, 9px on a tile, 10px before the status line, and the scrollbar thumb. Borders are 1px hairlines in Line (tiles, chips, cards, seams, rows) or Line Strong (group-title underline, resting fields, bar secondary buttons), with one 2px Line Strong rule on top of each new-patient step; the accent, ink, and outline buttons carry a 1.5px border that is transparent on the filled ones and Line Strong on the outline, so all three align. Photographs are clipped to their frame and never bordered.

### Status Line
`northline.js` holds the `HOURS` table (JS weekday keys, 24h decimal ranges, `null` for Saturday and Sunday) and `PHONES_UNTIL` (17.5), and derives the hero line from `new Date()`: "Open today until 5 pm · phones answered until 5:30 pm", "Opens at 7:30 am · text us anytime" before opening, or "Closed now · opens tomorrow at 7:30 am · text us anytime" (or the weekday) otherwise, always followed by " · Duluth". The same table marks today's row in the hours card and fills the Day select; the HTML table is the visible copy and the JS table is the copy the page reasons from. Keep both in sync.

### The Availability Board and Insurance Strip (signature)
The two answers, in the first viewport, and both wired to the form. `northline.js` holds `OPENINGS`, a fictional week of open chairs as decimal start times per weekday, and walks forward up to 21 days from `new Date()` to find the next three (skipping anything within 30 minutes of now on the first day). Each tile gets a day label ("Today", "Tomorrow", or "Thu Sep 17"), a time ("2:40 pm"), an ISO `data-date`, a `data-period` of "Morning (7:30–12)" or "Afternoon (12–5)", and an `aria-label` that ends "Request this time." Tapping a tile sets the Day and Time of day selects, scrolls to the form (smooth unless reduced motion), and after 500ms focuses "What's going on?"; "Start your forms" sets the reason to "New-patient exam & cleaning" if empty and focuses the Day select. Under the tiles, the insurance strip is one line: "In-network:" in 700, six chips, and "Don't see yours? Text us the card." with an SMS link; the same chips reappear as eight in the insurance section. On submit, the confirmation reads back the first name, the reason, the weekday and window, and adds a same-day line when the reason matches `hurts|broken` ("Because something hurts, we'll look for a same-day slot first"), then a per-plan note: the page prices for "No insurance", "text us a photo of your card" for "Another plan", or "We'll check your <plan> plan and text the estimated cost before the visit."

### Motion
One easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and three durations (160ms, 320ms, 700ms), gated behind a `.js` class so nothing is hidden without script. The hero title, status line, sub line, actions, board, and insurance strip rise 14px over 700ms with an 80ms stagger (0/80/160/240/320/400ms). The hero photograph drifts from `scale(1.04)` to 1 over 12s, once, from origin 50% 45%. Nothing else animates: the uniform section rise the brief described was removed in the finish round, so there is no reveal observer and the sections below the hero are static. Button hover, link underlines, tile borders, and field borders run at 160ms; nav chrome and the skip link at 320ms. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes every animation, collapses transitions, disables smooth scroll, and makes the board's scroll-to-form and focus instant.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every color in the section 1 token block and add a client's brand as a new `html[data-skin="<id>"]` block that redefines all of them, including `--accent-text`, `--accent-rgb`, `--tile`, `--tile-dot`, `--veil`, and `--chrome`; on a light ground set `--accent-text` deeper than `--accent`, keep `--tile` a white wash, and set `--veil` and `--chrome` to the ground.
- **Do** keep Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 700 with negative tracking for the wordmark, headlines, titles, step titles, and tile times only; keep body at 1.125rem, ledes to 1.3rem, and nothing read below 0.9375rem; keep weights to 400, 500, and 700.
- **Do** keep the hero's context below the H1 as a sentence-case status line in Teal Text, and lead a step with its title over a 2px rule, never a numeral.
- **Do** rule with ink hairlines (16% and 32%) and section with the three mist steps; pill every action and chip; fill the nav pill with ink and the hero pill with teal; cut tiles, cards, photos, and fields at 12px; point with a Tile Dot circle on tiles and chips.
- **Do** set tabular numerals on prices, tile times, the status line, and hours; price a common visit as one number with no prefix.
- **Do** keep photographs of light rooms in color under the mist veil, and keep the fixed bar's cast as the only shadow.
- **Do** keep Find a time, Call, and Forms in the fixed bar below 900px and the ink "Book online" in the nav above 960px, and keep the status line, the teal pill, the three tiles, and the insurance strip inside the hero.
- **Do** drive hours and openings from the one `HOURS` table and the one `OPENINGS` table: status line, today's row, the three tiles, and the Day select.
- **Do** carry `data-date` and `data-period` on every tile so the form is always prefilled, and keep the insurance select's options identical to the chips.
- **Do** cap running copy at 40 to 62ch, the H1 at 13ch, and H2s at 16ch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set `--accent` as text on a light skin, add a second accent, or put the fill teal on a tile's dot; don't use `--accent` where `--accent-text` is meant, or the reverse.
- **Don't** put a tracked-caps eyebrow above the H1 or an H2, a numeral on a step, or a weight of 600 anywhere.
- **Don't** add a radius other than the pill or 12px, a border in the accent outside a hovered tile, a focused field, and the status box, or a shadow on any surface, button, card, tile, or field.
- **Don't** veil a photograph with a tint other than the ground, put a shadow under the hero image, or use a photograph of a mouth, an instrument, or a smile.
- **Don't** add a native date input, "we accept most insurance" with no list, a PDF of forms, a phone number as the only next step, ratings, reviews, "voted best", or any proof device; Northline is fictional and the page must not fabricate.
- **Don't** write skin-specific selectors below the token block except `color-scheme`; if a skin needs one, it is a new base.
- **Don't** index this page: keep `noindex, nofollow` and keep it out of `sitemap.xml`.

## Reusing this base for a client

Northline is the base; a client site is a copy of `concepts/northline/` with these replaced.

- **Tokens:** either overwrite the `:root` block in `northline.css` (the client becomes the default) or add `html[data-skin="<client>"]` with all of the color tokens (accent, accent-text, accent-rgb, accent-ink, accent-hover, tile, tile-dot, and the neutrals), `--veil`, and `--chrome`, and set the attribute in the `<head>` script (or accept it via `?skin=`). Check Ink Soft, Ink Muted, and Teal Text contrast on the new ground; on a light ground the text accent must be deeper than the fill. The select chevron and the bar shadow are hard-coded in Sky's ink and need editing for a skin that is the default.
- **Photography:** the two images are Unsplash placeholders (`images.unsplash.com`, with `srcset`). Replace the hero with a light operatory or reception at the same crop (positioned 50% 45%) and the practice frame with a 4:3 room; keep them rooms, not mouths. Write real `alt` text for the practice photograph; the hero stays decorative.
- **Hours and openings:** edit `HOURS` in `northline.js` (keys 0 to 6, `[open, close]` in 24h decimal, `null` for closed), `PHONES_UNTIL`, `TOWN`, and the visible `<table data-hours>` rows, the footer hours line, and the phones copy in the hours note; replace `OPENINGS` with a feed from the practice software (Dentrix, Open Dental, Curve) so the tiles are real.
- **Prices and services:** the four price rows and their qualifiers, the four service rows, the services note (payment plan terms), and the reason select's options.
- **Insurance:** the six hero chips, the eight section chips, and the Insurance select's options, kept identical; the SMS number on "Text us the card".
- **Phone, address, dentist:** the `tel:` and `sms:` links (hero, insurance card, book lede, footer, bar), the address in visit and footer, the Maps `destination`, the practice copy and signature.
- **Copy:** `<title>`, `meta description`, the H1 and sub line, the six H2s and ledes, the four steps, the visit facts (parking, running late), and the wordmark text in the nav and footer.
- **Form endpoint:** replace `send()` in `northline.js` with a POST to the practice software or intake system, point "Start your forms" at the real intake, remove the "Concept form" note, and keep the validation, the open-days select, the urgent path, and the per-plan note.
- **Meta:** replace `theme-color` with the client's ground, remove `noindex` and the footer's fictional-practice line, add the page to the sitemap, and update the `color-scheme` meta if the default skin is dark.
