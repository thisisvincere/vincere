Your first visit, without the waiting room clipboard.

Most of the dread is not knowing what happens. So here is what happens.

  1. Forms on your phone

    About eight minutes, done from the couch. Nothing to print, nothing on a clipboard.

  2. Bring two things

    Your insurance card, if you have one, and a list of any medications. That's it.

  3. The first visit is about an hour

    X-rays, a look around, a cleaning if you want one, and a plain-language talk about anything we find. No treatment happens that day unless you ask for it.

  4. You're in charge of the pace

    Headphones, a blanket, numbing gel before any needle, and a raised hand stops everything, no questions. Sedation is available if you want it; just say so when you book.
