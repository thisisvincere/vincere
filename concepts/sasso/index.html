Sasso means stone.

The hearth at the back of the room is built from the same grey stone the building stood on in 1911. Everything we cook passes over it at some point.

We opened in 2019 with twelve tables, one pasta machine, and a list of things we refused to do: no music you have to talk over, no tasting menu, no dish you can't pronounce after a glass of wine. The menu changes with what the market has. The hearth doesn't.

It's the kind of place you come on a Tuesday. Bring someone.

— Marco & Lena Rossi, owners
