# Design System: Auburn & Ash

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The DM, Answered"**

Auburn & Ash is a one-page hair-salon site that answers the message every salon gets ("how much for X, and do you have anything Thursday?") before it is sent: the first screen carries the lowercase promise, whether the salon is open today and until when walk-ins are taken, a copper "Book a chair", and the three prices the home-page preview promised; the full menu prices color honestly "from"; four stylists are shown by their work with a button that books that person; hours, parking, and the cancellation policy are stated before anyone has to ask. The world is a soft sage room with a green door: sage ground, deep green-black ink, and one copper that is worn by the action pill, the ampersand in the wordmark, the dots on price rows, and the "from" on color prices. Photography is monochrome, so copper is the only chroma on the page. Remove the copy and you would still recognize it: sage ground, one copper pill, a lowercase medium-weight headline at tight tracking, dotted price rows with copper "from".

It is VINCERE's fourth template base ("one structure, many skins") and the sibling of Sasso, Apex, and Kestrel. Every color on the page lives in one token block at the top of `auburn.css`; a skin is a second, complete token block on `html[data-skin]` and nothing structural changes beneath it. Two alternate skins ship (Blush, Noir) and match the three-way switcher in the VINCERE home page's concept panel. Like Kestrel, the accent is split into a fill (`--accent`) and what copper wears as running text (`--accent-text`), deeper on the two light skins so it clears contrast on the ground and identical on Noir; the `--veil` and `--chrome` triplets equal the ground so the hero veil and the nav dissolve into the page. Auburn & Ash is fictional, carries `noindex, nofollow`, and is never listed in the sitemap; its palette and type are deliberately not VINCERE tokens (see the root DESIGN.md's "Concept Previews" exception).

Density is that of a printed service menu: sections breathe on a clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem) rhythm, copy is capped at 40 to 62ch, and prices sit on hairline rows with a copper dot rather than in cards. Only three things are raised surfaces (fields, the four stylist cards, the hours card), and only photographs carry a shadow. Confirmed rejections: a full-bleed color moodboard hero, "DM for prices", a Booksy button as the only content, a gallery of headshots with no way to book the person in them, reviews or ratings, and a tracked-caps eyebrow above any headline.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three sage steps (#E9EDE4, #DDE3D8, #F5F7F2) do the sectioning; hairlines at 16% and 32% ink do the ruling (18% and 34% on Noir)
- One copper in two roles: #B4552D as fill (the pill, the dots, selection, the caret, the status-box wash and focus halo) and #9A4622 as text (the ampersand, the status line, the "from", stylist roles, today's hours row, link hover); Noir uses one value for both
- Figtree 500 at -0.035 to -0.045em for the lowercase H1, every lowercase H2, group titles, stylist names, the hours title, and the wordmark; Figtree 400 to 600 for everything read, at 1.0625rem base; 600 is the ceiling
- Two radii: pills (999px) on every action and chip, 12px on cards, photos, fields, and the status box; circles only as 7px and 9px dots
- One shadow, under photographs only: the 4:5 stylist frames carry `0 18px 40px -22px` at 35% ink; no surface, button, or card is lifted
- Monochrome photography: the hero interior is a black-and-white image under a sage veil; the four stylist frames render through `grayscale(1) contrast(1.05)` so they read as one set
- Tabular numerals on prices, the status line, the hours table, and the footer hours; copper "from" only on color prices
- A skin is a token block on `html[data-skin]`, selected by `?skin=<id>`, with the accent split into fill and text and the veil equal to the ground
- One easing (out-expo) and three durations (160/320/700ms); the hero rises in an 80ms stagger and the hero photograph drifts 4% over 12s

## Colors

A soft, low-chroma palette where three sage steps carry structure, green-black ink carries every word and number, and one copper carries the pill a would-be DM is looking for; photographs are monochrome so the copper is the only color on the page.

### Primary
- **Copper** (`{colors.accent}`): the fill accent. Fills "Book a chair" in the hero, the nav, the form submit ("Request this chair"), and the mobile bar; is the 7px dot before every price row and the 9px dot before the status line when open; is `accent-color`, `caret-color`, and text selection; borders the sent-form status box and washes it (8%) and the field focus halo (22%) through the `--accent-rgb` triplet. Not used as text on the light skins.
- **Copper Text** (`{colors.accent-text}`): what copper wears as running text on the ground: the ampersand in "Auburn & Ash", the live status line, the "from" before a color price, each stylist's role line, today's row in the hours table, a link or a price name on hover, and the global focus ring. Marked in the build at 5.4:1 on the sage ground. Split from the fill so a light skin can deepen the text without darkening the pill; Noir sets both to the same tan.
- **Copper Ink** (`{colors.accent-ink}`): text on the copper fill, a warm off-white marked 4.8:1 on the pill.
- **Copper Hover** (`{colors.accent-hover}`): the darker copper the accent pill turns on hover, with a 1px rise.
- **Focus** (`{colors.focus}`): the global 2px focus outline at 3px offset, cut at 12px. Copper Text on the light skins; the tan on Noir.

### Neutral
- **Sage** (`{colors.ground}`): page ground, the `theme-color`, and the source of the `--veil` and `--chrome` triplets (233, 237, 228) that build the hero veil, the nav, and the mobile bar.
- **Sage Deep** (`{colors.ground-2}`): the alternating band (stylists, visit), ruled top and bottom with Line; also the ground behind a loading stylist photo.
- **Sage Light** (`{colors.ground-3}`): raised surfaces: form fields, the four stylist cards, the hours card.
- **Ink** (`{colors.ink}`): headings, body, the wordmark, price names and prices, field text, the outline button's text and hover border, facts' terms, and the status box's text. A deep green-black, not a neutral grey.
- **Ink Soft** (`{colors.ink-soft}`): ledes, nav links, the hero sub line, stylist bios, chips, hours values, facts' definitions, the footer, and the field hover border. Marked 6.8:1 on Sage.
- **Ink Muted** (`{colors.ink-muted}`): notes, fine print, placeholders, the "Optional" mark, the row qualifier (`wash included`, `under 12`, `updo or set`), the select chevron, the concept credit, the demo note, and the status dot when closed. Marked 5.1:1 on Sage and 4.7:1 on Sage Deep.
- **Line** and **Line Strong** (`{colors.line}`, `{colors.line-strong}`): 16% and 32% ink. Line rules price rows, hours rows, band seams, stylist and hours card borders, the stuck nav, the bar's top, the footer, and the footer's concept seam; Line Strong underlines group titles and text links, borders every field, chip, outline button, and bar button at rest, and colors the scrollbar thumb.
- **Error** (`{colors.error}`): a brick red for invalid field borders, error messages, and the status box's error border.

### Skins
A skin is a second token block on `html[data-skin="<id>"]` that redefines the same names. `?skin=blush` or `?skin=noir` on the URL sets the attribute from an inline script in `<head>`, before first paint, so the home page's "Open the full concept" link opens the look the visitor chose in the switcher. The veil and chrome triplets always equal the skin's ground.

- **Blush** (`{colors.blush-*}`): warm pink-beige grounds, plum-brown ink, and a terracotta red in place of the copper: fill `blush-accent` with a warm off-white label, text `blush-accent-text` (5.6:1 on the ground), hover a darker red. Hairlines stay at 16% and 32% of the plum ink. Light; `color-scheme` stays light.
- **Noir** (`{colors.noir-*}`): near-black warm grounds, bone ink, and a tan in place of the copper that is both fill and text (`noir-accent-text` 8.1:1 on the ground) with near-black on it; hover a paler tan. Hairlines lift to 18% and 34% bone; `color-scheme` flips to dark, the error softens to salmon, and the photo shadow deepens to 60% black. The only dark skin.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Copper Rule.** Every use of the accent names which role it is: `--accent` on a fill with `--accent-ink` on it, `--accent-text` as text on the ground. On a light skin the text value is deeper than the fill, so the same copper clears contrast in a word that it clears in a pill; a light skin that sets `color: var(--accent)` on running text is a bug. A dark skin may set both to one value.

**The Veil Is the Ground Rule.** `--veil` and `--chrome` are the ground as an RGB triplet, never a separate tint. The hero veil fades the photograph into the page, and the nav and mobile bar are the page at 60% and 90% opacity with blur. A skin that changes its ground changes all three.

**The Token Block Rule.** Every color on the page resolves to a token in section 1 of `auburn.css`. A skin redefines that block and nothing else; the only skin-scoped selector below it is `color-scheme`. A brand that needs a structural override is a new base, not a skin.

**The One Chroma Rule.** Copper is the only saturated color on the page; photographs are monochrome (the hero interior by choice of image, the stylist frames through a grayscale filter) so nothing competes with it. Skins swap the copper, never add a second accent.

**The Sage Hairline Rule.** Rules are 1px of ink at 16% (Line) or 32% (Line Strong); there are no solid divider colors and no border in the accent except the sent-form status box. The dot does the pointing; the line does the ruling.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Figtree (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial)
**Body Font:** Figtree (same stack)

One family, loaded from Google Fonts at 400, 500, 600 only.

**Character:** One friendly geometric in two pressures. At 500 with tight negative tracking and all lowercase it is the salon's own voice: unhurried, a little wry, never shouting; the heaviest thing on the page is medium weight. At 400 to 600 it does all the reading, the labeling, and the pricing. 600 is the ceiling; there is no bold on the page.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (500, clamp(3rem, 9vw, 6.75rem), 0.96, -0.045em, lowercase): the hero H1 only ("good hair, no drama."), capped at 12ch, balanced.
- **Headline** (500, clamp(2.2rem, 4.8vw, 3.6rem), 0.98, -0.04em, lowercase): section H2s ("prices, in the open.", "who's behind the chair.", "book a chair.", "find us."), capped at 16ch, balanced. Each ends in a period.
- **Title** (500, clamp(1.5rem, 2.4vw, 2rem), -0.035em, lowercase): price group titles over a Line Strong rule.
- **Card Title** (500, 1.5rem, 1.05, -0.035em): stylist names, in title case because they are names; 1.35rem from 1000px when the grid is 4-up.
- **Hours Title** (500, 1.6rem, -0.035em, lowercase): "hours" in the hours card.
- **Wordmark** (500, 1.25rem, -0.01em): "Auburn & Ash" in the nav and footer with the ampersand in Copper Text, upright. Shown at every width.
- **Status Line** (600, 1.0625rem, tabular, Copper Text): the live line under the H1, led by a 9px copper dot.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(1.1rem, 1.4vw, 1.25rem), 1.55): the paragraph under each H2, Ink Soft, capped at 46ch (40ch when it sits beside the heading from 900px). The hero's sub line is clamp(1.05rem, 1.5vw, 1.2rem) in Ink Soft.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): the base; price rows and the form inherit it. Stylist roles, bios, chips' neighbors, nav links, the menu note, facts, book fine print, footer, and bar buttons run at Body Small (0.9375rem).
- **Button** (600, 1rem, line-height 1): every button; `btn--sm` is 0.875rem.
- **Role** (600, 0.9375rem, Copper Text): each stylist's specialty line under the name.
- **Chip** (500, 0.8125rem, Ink Soft): the three service chips on each stylist card.
- **Field Label** (600, 0.875rem): above every field, with an optional Label-style "Optional" mark.
- **Label** (500, 0.75rem, 0.06em, uppercase, Ink Muted): the " · today" suffix in the hours table and the "Optional" mark on a field. The only tracked uppercase on the page, and it never precedes a headline.
- **Caption** (400, 0.8125rem, Ink Muted): the demo note, field errors (in Error), and the concept credit. The hours note runs at 0.875rem.

### Named Rules
**The Lowercase Medium Rule.** Figtree 500 with negative tracking sets the wordmark, the H1, every H2, group titles, stylist names, and the hours title, and every heading except a stylist's name is lowercase and ends in a period. It never sets a paragraph, a label, a button, or a link. Nothing on the page is heavier than 600.

**The Sentence-Case Signal Rule.** The hero's context sits below the H1 as a sentence-case status line in Copper Text, never above it as a tracked-caps eyebrow. A stylist's card leads with the name, then the role in copper, never a label.

**The Tabular Rule.** Anything with a digit that lines up (prices, the status line, the hours table, the footer hours) sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. "from" is a 600-weight `em` at 0.85em in Copper Text before a color price and nowhere else; cuts, blowouts, and treatments are one number with no prefix.

## Layout

A single 1120px wrap (`min(100% - 2 * gutter, 1120px)`) with a fluid gutter (clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)) and a fluid section rhythm (clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem)). Bands alternate Sage (menu, book) and Sage Deep (stylists, visit) with a Line hairline at each seam. The nav is sticky at 72px on 60% chrome; `scroll-padding-top` is 84px. The hero pulls up under the nav (`margin-top: -72px`, `padding-top: 72px`), is `min(100svh, 900px)` tall, and aligns its content to the bottom, padded clamp(3rem, 8vw, 6rem) above and clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 5rem) below.

The hero stacks H1, status line, sub line, the actions row (copper pill and text link, wrapping at gap 1.25rem 1.75rem), and the three-row price teaser at max 520px, all bottom-left over the photograph. The photograph is positioned at 70% 50% so the veil's clear side shows the room's right half; the veil is two gradients, vertical 35% → 15% at 28% → 86% at 58% → 100% at the bottom, and horizontal 70% → 35% at 45% → 0 at 80%, both in the ground triplet.

Breakpoints are content-specific: 600px (stylist cards 2-up; form rows 1fr/1fr; the form foot splits button and note auto/1fr), 700px (nav links appear), 760px (price groups 2-up), 900px (nav "Book a chair" appears; mobile bar hides and the body's bottom padding drops; menu and stylists heads go 1fr/1fr with the lede right-aligned at 40ch; book 0.8fr/1.2fr; visit 1.1fr/0.9fr with the hours card sticky at 96px; footer 1.2fr/1fr with the concept line spanning both), and 1000px (stylist cards 4-up, names 1.35rem). Below 900px the body reserves `64px + safe-area-inset-bottom` for the fixed action bar (Book / Call / Directions, 1.4fr/1fr/1fr).

Photography is five Unsplash placeholders: the hero interior (monochrome, `object-fit: cover`, with `srcset` at 900/1600/2400) and four 4:5 stylist frames (500/800/1200, lazy) showing hair being done rather than headshots. Stylist cards are `grid-template-rows: auto 1fr` with the body `align-content: start`, so the four frames line up and the text stacks from the top. Grid children carry `min-width: 0` so an image's intrinsic width cannot blow out a column; price names ellipsize rather than wrap.

## Elevation & Depth

Nearly flat. Depth comes from the three sage steps (page, band, light surface) and from ink hairlines; no button, card, field, or nav carries a shadow. The one elevation shadow on the page is the soft offset under each stylist photograph, which sits inside its card like a print laid on a counter. The hero photograph carries no shadow: it is under the page, not on it, and the veil does the blending. Nav and bar are translucent chrome (60% resting, 90% stuck or fixed) with `blur(14px)`. One zero-offset ring exists and is state, not depth: the field focus halo in copper.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Photo** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -22px rgba(31, 42, 34, 0.35)`): the four stylist frames only, through `--shadow`. Follows the skin: plum ink on Blush, 60% black on Noir.
- **Field focus** (`0 0 0 3px rgba(var(--accent-rgb), 0.22)` with a Copper border): the only accent halo, and it is state, not elevation. `--accent-rgb` follows the skin.
- **Focus ring** (`outline: 2px solid var(--focus); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 12px`): the global keyboard ring in Copper Text (tan on Noir).

### Named Rules
**The Print on the Counter Rule.** A photograph is the only thing that casts a shadow, and only when it sits inside a card. Surfaces are flat at rest and flat on hover; hover is a 1px rise, a border change, a fill change, or a color change, never a shadow.

**The Monochrome Photo Rule.** A photograph is black and white. The hero interior is chosen monochrome and sits under the sage veil; a stylist frame is forced through `grayscale(1) contrast(1.05)` so four different sources read as one set. Copper stays the only chroma. Both are Unsplash placeholders the client replaces, and the replacement inherits the filter.

## Shapes

Two radii and a circle. Every action and chip is a pill (999px): the copper "Book a chair" in the hero, nav, form, and bar; the outline "Book with <name>" on each card; the bar's Call and Directions; the skip link; the service chips. Every surface and field is cut at 12px (`--radius`): stylist cards, the hours card, inputs and selects, the status box, and the focus outline; the photograph inside a card is cut 4px tighter (8px) so the corners nest. Circles appear only as dots: 7px before price rows, 9px before the status line, and the scrollbar thumb. Borders are 1px hairlines in Line (surfaces, seams, rows) or Line Strong (group-title underline, resting fields, chips, bar secondary buttons); the accent and outline buttons carry a 1.5px border that is transparent on the accent and Line Strong on the outline, so both align. Photographs are clipped to their frame and never bordered.

## Components

### Buttons
Round, medium-weight, and one of them copper.
- **Shape:** pill (999px), 1.5px border, 1rem/1.6rem padding at 1rem 600 with a 0.5rem gap (`btn--sm`: 0.7rem/1.1rem at 0.875rem).
- **Accent:** Copper Ink on Copper. Used for "Book a chair" in the hero, the nav (from 900px), and the mobile bar, and for the form submit ("Request this chair", which reads "Sending…" while disabled). Hover to Copper Hover with a 1px rise; active presses 1px down.
- **Outline:** transparent on a Line Strong border, Ink text; hover turns the border Ink. Used small for "Book with <name>" on every stylist card.
- **Focus:** the global 2px Focus outline at 3px offset.
- **Text link:** inherits color, 1px underline in Line Strong at 0.2em offset; hover turns the text Copper Text and the underline currentColor. Used for "See prices" beside the hero pill, the phone, Maps, Instagram, and the VINCERE credit.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 1px Line Strong border, transparent fill, 0.35rem/0.7rem, 0.8125rem 500 Ink Soft, `white-space: nowrap`.
- **State:** static; three per stylist card naming the services that stylist books most. Not filters.

### Dots and Rows
- **Dot:** a filled circle in Copper before a row item: 7px on price rows, 9px on the status line (Ink Muted when closed). It is the page's bullet and its only ornament.
- **Price row:** a `<button>` styled as a flex row on a Line rule at 0.85rem vertical padding, `white-space: nowrap`, full width: a 7px copper dot, the name in Ink (ellipsized, with an upright Ink Muted qualifier `em` at 0.9em such as `wash included` or `under 12`), and the price pushed to the right edge in Ink 600 tabular. Color prices carry a 600-weight "from" `em` in Copper Text at 0.85em; every other price carries nothing. Hover turns the name Copper Text. The same row serves the hero teaser (three rows at max 520px) and the four price groups (500 lowercase title over a Line Strong rule, 2-up from 760px). The note runs at 0.9375rem Ink Muted, 62ch, with a 600 Ink lead.

### Cards / Containers
- **Stylist card:** Sage Light, 1px Line, 12px radius, 1rem padding, no shadow; `auto 1fr` rows. Holds the 4:5 photo frame (8px radius, Photo shadow, Sage Deep ground while loading, image grayscaled), then a body at `align-content: start` with gap 0.5rem: name (Card Title), role (600 Copper Text), bio (Body Small Ink Soft), three chips, and the small outline "Book with <name>" button justified start. 1-up, 2-up from 600px, 4-up from 1000px.
- **Hours card:** Sage Light, 1px Line, 12px radius, clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.25rem) padding, no shadow; sticky at 96px from 900px. Holds a 500 lowercase title at 1.6rem, a two-column table with Line rules and tabular figures, values right-aligned in Ink Soft, and a note in Ink Muted at 0.875rem with a 600 Ink lead. The row matching `new Date().getDay()` gets `is-today`: Copper Text, 600, and a " · today" suffix in the Label style.
- **Status box:** 1rem/1.25rem, Copper border on an 8% copper wash, Ink text, 12px radius; error state swaps to an Error border. On success the form hides its rows and focuses the box.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Sage Light fill, 1px Line Strong border, 12px radius, 0.9rem/1.1rem padding, 3.25rem min height, inherits body type; label above at 0.875rem 600 with an optional Label-style "Optional" mark in Ink Muted.
- **Hover:** border to Ink Soft.
- **Focus:** border to Copper, 3px copper halo at 22%, no outline.
- **Error:** Error border, message below at 0.8125rem in Error; the field also sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. The phone field wants ten digits; the day must be an open day; every other required field wants a value. Errors appear on blur once a field has content, on submit for all, and clear as the visitor types or changes a select.
- **Select:** native appearance removed, an Ink Muted chevron SVG at right 1rem, placeholder option in Ink Muted. Service is grouped by the four menu headings; Stylist offers "First available" plus the four names; Time of day is three windows; Day is a select of the next ten open days filled by `auburn.js` (the first reads "Today, …" when today is open), so a closed day cannot be chosen. There is no native date input.

### Navigation
- **Style:** sticky 72px bar on 60% chrome with `blur(14px) saturate(140%)`; after 24px of scroll (`is-stuck`) it deepens to 90% and gains a Line bottom rule. Wordmark left ("Auburn & Ash", 500, copper ampersand, shown at every width), three links (Prices, Stylists, Visit) at 0.9375rem 500 Ink Soft hovering to Copper Text from 700px, small copper "Book a chair" pill right from 900px.
- **Mobile action bar:** fixed to the bottom on 90% chrome with a Line top rule and no shadow, safe-area padded, 48px pills at 0.9375rem 600: Book a chair (copper), Call and Directions (Ink on a 1.5px Line Strong border). Present from first paint; hidden from 900px.

### Status Line
`auburn.js` holds the `HOURS` table (JS weekday keys, 24h decimal ranges, `null` for Sunday and Monday) and `WALK_IN_UNTIL` (17), and derives the hero line from `new Date()`: "Open today until 7 pm · walk-ins until 5 pm" (or "· booked chairs only tonight" after the cutoff), "Opens at 10 am · walk-ins until 5 pm" before opening, or "Closed now · opens tomorrow at 10 am" (or the weekday) otherwise, always followed by " · Haywood Rd". It is Copper Text 600 tabular at 1.0625rem with a 9px copper dot that turns Ink Muted when closed. It sits under the H1 with `aria-live="polite"`, and before script runs it reads "Tuesday to Saturday · Haywood Rd". The same table marks today's row in the hours card and fills the Day select; the HTML table is the visible copy and the JS table is the copy the page reasons from. Keep both in sync.

### The Chair (signature)
A stylist card whose action books that person, and a price row that books that service. Every "Book with <name>" button carries `data-stylist` and `data-service` (the stylist's first specialty); every price row in the teaser and the menu carries `data-service`. Tapping either sets the matching select in the form, scrolls to the form (smooth unless reduced motion), and after 500ms moves focus to the next empty field: the Day select after a stylist, the Stylist select after a service. The visitor arrives with the two hard questions already answered and the price they tapped still in view above. On submit, the confirmation reads back the service, the stylist ("the first available stylist" when none was chosen), the weekday and window, and for a "from" service (`FROM_SERVICES`: single-process, balayage, partial highlights) adds "and the exact color price at your consult".

### Motion
One easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and three durations (160ms, 320ms, 700ms), gated behind a `.js` class so nothing is hidden without script. The hero title, status line, sub line, actions, and teaser rise 14px over 700ms with an 80ms stagger (0/80/160/240/320ms). The hero photograph drifts from `scale(1.04)` to 1 over 12s, once. The menu head and groups, stylists head and cards, book copy and form, visit copy, and hours card rise 12px over 700ms once, on intersection (8% threshold, -8% bottom margin), staggered 60ms in groups of four. Button hover, link underlines, and field borders run at 160ms; nav chrome and the skip link at 320ms. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes every animation, collapses transitions, disables smooth scroll, and skips the reveal observer.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every color in the section 1 token block and add a client's brand as a new `html[data-skin="<id>"]` block that redefines all of them, including `--accent-text`, `--accent-rgb`, `--veil`, `--chrome`, and `--shadow`; on a light ground set `--accent-text` deeper than `--accent`, and set `--veil` and `--chrome` to the ground.
- **Do** keep Figtree 500 with negative tracking for the wordmark, headlines, titles, and stylist names only, keep headings lowercase with a period, and keep 600 as the ceiling for anything read.
- **Do** keep the hero's context below the H1 as a sentence-case status line in Copper Text, and lead a stylist card with the name and then the role.
- **Do** rule with ink hairlines (16% and 32%) and section with the three sage steps; pill every action and chip; cut surfaces, photos, and fields at 12px; point with a 7px copper dot.
- **Do** set tabular numerals on prices, the status line, and hours, and put a copper "from" only on color prices.
- **Do** keep photography monochrome: choose a black-and-white hero and leave the grayscale filter on stylist frames; keep the photo shadow as the only shadow.
- **Do** keep Book, Call, and Directions in the fixed bar below 900px and the copper "Book a chair" in the nav above it, and keep the status line, the pill, and the three teaser prices inside the hero.
- **Do** drive hours from the one `HOURS` table: status line, today's row, and the Day select.
- **Do** carry `data-stylist` and `data-service` on every card button and price row so the form is always prefilled.
- **Do** cap running copy at 40 to 62ch, the H1 at 12ch, and H2s at 16ch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set `--accent` as text on a light skin, add a second accent, or let a photograph carry color; don't use `--accent` where `--accent-text` is meant, or the reverse.
- **Don't** put a tracked-caps eyebrow above the H1 or an H2, and don't set a heading in title case or with a weight above 500 (names excepted).
- **Don't** add a radius other than the pill or 12px (8px inside a card), a border in the accent outside the status box, or a shadow on any surface, button, card, or field.
- **Don't** veil a photograph with a tint other than the ground, and don't put a shadow under the hero image.
- **Don't** add a native date input, a headshot gallery, "DM for prices", a third-party booking button as the only path, ratings, reviews, or any proof device; Auburn & Ash is fictional and the page must not fabricate.
- **Don't** write skin-specific selectors below the token block except `color-scheme`; if a skin needs one, it is a new base.
- **Don't** index this page: keep `noindex, nofollow` and keep it out of `sitemap.xml`.

## Reusing this base for a client

Auburn & Ash is the base; a client site is a copy of `concepts/auburn/` with these replaced.

- **Tokens:** either overwrite the `:root` block in `auburn.css` (the client becomes the default) or add `html[data-skin="<client>"]` with all of the color tokens (accent, accent-text, accent-rgb, accent-ink, accent-hover, and the neutrals), `--veil`, `--chrome`, and `--shadow`, and set the attribute in the `<head>` script (or accept it via `?skin=`). Check Ink Soft, Ink Muted, and Copper Text contrast on the new ground; on a light ground the text copper must be deeper than the fill.
- **Photography:** the five images are Unsplash placeholders (`images.unsplash.com`, with `srcset`). Replace the hero with a monochrome interior at the same crop (positioned 70% 50%), and the four stylist frames with 4:5 shots of work at the chair; the grayscale filter stays so the set stays one set. Write real `alt` text for each.
- **Hours:** edit `HOURS` in `auburn.js` (keys 0 to 6, `[open, close]` in 24h decimal, `null` for closed), `WALK_IN_UNTIL`, `STREET`, and the visible `<table data-hours>` rows, the footer hours line, and the walk-in copy in the hero, book fine print, visit facts, and hours note.
- **Menu and stylists:** the price rows (and their `data-service`), the Service select's optgroups, `FROM_SERVICES`, the four cards (name, role, bio, chips, `data-stylist`, `data-service`), and the Stylist select's options. Keep names identical across card button, select option, and confirmation.
- **Phone, address, Instagram:** the `tel:` links (book lede, footer, bar), the address in visit and footer, the Maps `destination` (visit and bar), and the Instagram handle.
- **Copy:** `<title>`, `meta description`, the H1 and sub line, the four H2s and ledes, the menu note, the visit facts (parking, walk-ins, cancellations), and the wordmark text in the nav and footer.
- **Form endpoint:** replace `send()` in `auburn.js` with a POST to the booking system (Square Appointments, Vagaro, Boulevard) or the front desk's phone, remove the "Concept form" note, and keep the validation, the open-days select, and the color-price note.
- **Meta:** replace `theme-color` with the client's ground, remove `noindex` and the footer's fictional-salon line, add the page to the sitemap, and update the `color-scheme` meta if the default skin is dark.
