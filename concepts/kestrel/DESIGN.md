# Design System: Kestrel Plumbing & Heating

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Dispatcher Picking Up"**

Kestrel is a one-page plumbing-and-heating site that behaves like a dispatcher answering the phone: the first screen says whether someone is on shift and when the next truck can roll, puts Call and Text under the thumb, shows the license and insurance line, and posts the price of the three most common jobs, all before a single sentence of pitch. The world is a printed work order on a clipboard: warm off-white paper, near-black ink, and one high-visibility yellow that is only ever a block of fill (the Call button, the emergency band, the dots on price rows, the mobile bar). There is no hero photograph by decision; the page paints instantly and the yellow button is the picture. Remove the copy and you would still recognize it: off-white ground, one yellow block, heavy black type, dotted price rows.

It is VINCERE's third template base ("one structure, many skins") and the sibling of Sasso and Apex. Every color on the page lives in one token block at the top of `kestrel.css`; a skin is a second, complete token block on `html[data-skin]` and nothing structural changes beneath it. Two alternate skins ship (Navy, Forest) and match the three-way switcher in the VINCERE home page's concept panel. What Kestrel adds to the Apex pattern is a fill/text split with a separate band pair: `--accent` for fills, `--accent-text` for what signal text wears on the ground, and `--band` / `--band-ink` for the one solid block. On the default Safety skin `--accent-text` resolves to ink, because the yellow cannot clear contrast as text on the light ground; on Navy the same yellow is both fill and text; Forest swaps the yellow for two greens. Kestrel is fictional, carries `noindex, nofollow`, and is never listed in the sitemap; its palette and type are deliberately not VINCERE tokens.

Density is that of a rate card: sections breathe on a clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem) rhythm, copy is capped at 40 to 62ch, and data (jobs, steps, towns, hours, license facts) sits on hairline rows with a yellow dot rather than in cards. The surfaces are flat: no shadow is used for depth anywhere, and the only box-shadow on the page is the lift under the fixed mobile bar. Confirmed rejections: a slideshow of smiling vans, "call for a free estimate" with no prices, a wall of badges, reviews or ratings, a contact form as the only way in, and a tracked-caps eyebrow above any headline.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three paper steps (#F6F5F0, #ECEBE4, #FFFFFF) do the sectioning; hairlines at 16% and 32% ink do the ruling
- One yellow (#FFC531) worn only as fill: the Call button, the emergency band, the 7px and 8px dots, the mobile bar, text selection; on the light ground it is never text, and signal text (prices, step numbers, the dispatch line) wears ink through `--accent-text`
- Public Sans 800 at -0.02 to -0.04em for the wordmark, every headline, band title, group title, step title, step counter, and hours title; Public Sans 400 to 700 for everything read, at 1.0625rem base
- One radius: 6px on every control, field, surface, and photo; no pills; circles only as 7px, 8px, and 10px dots
- Flat surfaces: no elevation shadow anywhere except the fixed mobile bar; photographs are cut to 6px and carry nothing
- Tabular numerals on every number: prices, the dispatch line, the trust strip, the hours table, the license box, the footer, step counters, and every button
- A skin is a token block on `html[data-skin]`, selected by `?skin=<id>`, with the accent split into fill, text, and band
- One easing (out-expo) and three durations (160/320/700ms); the hero rises in an 80ms stagger; no hero drift because there is no hero photograph

## Colors

A warm, light palette where three paper steps carry structure, near-black ink carries every word and number, and one high-visibility yellow carries the block a homeowner's thumb is looking for.

### Primary
- **Safety Yellow** (`{colors.accent}`): the fill accent. Fills the "Call (970) 555-0147" button in the hero, the nav's "Call now", the form's submit, and the mobile bar's Call; is the 7px dot before each trust item and town, the 8px dot before each price row, the 10px dispatch dot when open, text selection, `accent-color`; washes the sent-form status box (18%) and the field focus halo (45%) through the `--accent-rgb` triplet. Marked in the build at 10.7:1 with ink on it.
- **Signal Text** (`{colors.accent-text}`): what signal text wears on the ground: the dispatch line, the price column, the step counters. On Safety it is ink, because the yellow is fill-only on the light ground; Navy sets it to the yellow (9.7:1 on navy) and Forest to the darker green. Split from the fill so a skin can decide whether the accent may speak as text.
- **Accent Ink** (`{colors.accent-ink}`): text on the yellow fill; ink on Safety and navy on Navy, white on Forest's green.
- **Accent Hover** (`{colors.accent-hover}`): the paler yellow the accent button turns on hover, with a 1px rise.
- **Band** and **Band Ink** (`{colors.band}`, `{colors.band-ink}`): the emergency band's ground and text. Set separately from the accent so a skin can keep the button and change the block (or the reverse); on all three shipped skins the band equals the accent. The band's button inverts them: Band Ink fill, Band text.
- **Focus** (`{colors.focus}`): the global 2px focus outline at 3px offset, cut at 6px. Ink on the light skins (Forest: the text green); the yellow on Navy.

- **White** (`{colors.ground-3}`): raised surfaces: form fields, the hours card, the license box, the photo placeholder ground.
- **Ink** (`{colors.ink}`): headings, body, the wordmark, job names, trust facts, the ink button's fill, the focus ring, the field's focused border, and the status box's border.
- **Ink Soft** (`{colors.ink-soft}`): ledes, nav links, step text, about paragraphs, towns, hours values, the trust strip, the footer, the field hover border. Marked 8.1:1 on Paper.
- **Ink Muted** (`{colors.ink-muted}`): notes, fine print, placeholders, the "Optional" mark, the "from" prefix, the job qualifier (`24/7`, `40–50 gal`, `credited to the repair`), the teaser caption, the " · today" suffix, the select chevron, the concept credit, and the dispatch dot when closed. - **Error** (`{colors.error}`): a brick red for invalid field borders, error messages, and the status box's error border.

### Skins
A skin is a second token block on `html[data-skin="<id>"]` that redefines the same names. `?skin=navy` or `?skin=forest` on the URL sets the attribute from an inline script in `<head>`, before first paint, so the home page's "Open the full concept" link opens the look the visitor chose in the switcher.

- **Navy** (`{colors.navy-*}`): deep navy grounds, cool white ink, the same yellow as both fill and text (`navy-accent-text` 9.7:1 on the ground) and as the band. Hairlines lift to 18% and 34% ink. `color-scheme` flips to dark, the error softens to salmon, and the focus ring becomes the yellow. The only dark skin, and the only one where the yellow speaks as text.
- **Forest** (`{colors.forest-*}`): pale green-grey grounds, deep green ink, and two greens in place of the yellow: a fill green (`forest-accent`, white label at 5.3:1) that is also the band, and a darker text green (`forest-accent-text`, 5.7:1 on the ground) that is also the hover and the focus ring. The one skin-scoped selector below the token block re-asserts the band button's text color.

### Named Rules
**The Fill-Only Yellow Rule.** On a light ground the yellow is a block, a dot, or a bar, never a letter. Signal text (dispatch line, prices, counters) goes through `--accent-text`, which Safety and Forest resolve to a dark value. A new element that sets `color: var(--accent)` on Safety is a bug.

**The Two-Pair Accent Rule.** Every use of the accent names which role it is: `--accent` on a fill with `--accent-ink` on it, `--accent-text` as text on the ground, `--band` with `--band-ink` for the one solid block. A skin may set all of them to two values (Navy) or three (Safety, Forest); a light skin must be able to make `--accent-text` dark.

**The Token Block Rule.** Every color on the page resolves to a token in section 1 of `kestrel.css`. A skin redefines that block and nothing else; the only skin-scoped selectors below it are `color-scheme` and the Forest band button's text. A brand that needs a structural override is a new base, not a skin.

**The Paper Hairline Rule.** Rules are 1px of ink at 16% (Line) or 32% (Line Strong); there are no solid divider colors and no border in the accent anywhere. The dot does the pointing; the line does the ruling.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Public Sans (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial)
**Body Font:** Public Sans (same stack)

One family, loaded from Google Fonts at 400, 500, 600, 700, 800.

**Character:** One grotesque in two voices. At 800 with tight negative tracking it is the stencil on the truck door: short, heavy, and never more than 16 characters wide. At 400 to 700 it does all the reading, the labeling, and the pricing. The pairing reads like a work order: one hand, two pressures.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(3rem, 9vw, 6.5rem), 0.96, -0.04em): the hero H1 only, capped at 16ch, balanced; drops to a fixed 2.5rem below 480px so the first answer fits one phone viewport.
- **Headline** (800, clamp(2.1rem, 4.6vw, 3.4rem), 1.02, -0.03em): section H2s, capped at 16ch, balanced.
- **Band Title** (800, clamp(1.4rem, 2.6vw, 2rem), 1.1, -0.03em): the emergency band's H2 in Band Ink.
- **Title** (800, clamp(1.35rem, 2.2vw, 1.75rem), -0.025em): price group titles over a Line Strong rule; the hours title is the same face at a fixed 1.5rem.
- **Step Title** (800, 1.25rem, 1.1, -0.02em): each step's heading; the step counter is the same face at 1.35rem in Signal Text with `decimal-leading-zero`, tabular.
- **Wordmark** (800, 1.2rem, -0.02em, uppercase): "KESTREL" in the nav and footer, with "Plumbing & Heating" beside it at 500, 0.9375rem, 60% opacity, baseline-aligned. Shown at every width.
- **Dispatch Line** (600, 1.0625rem, tabular, Signal Text): the live line under the H1, led by a 10px dot; 1rem below 480px.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(1.1rem, 1.4vw, 1.25rem), 1.55): the paragraph under each H2, Ink Soft, capped at 46ch (40ch in the prices head); the hero lede is one sentence at clamp(1.05rem, 1.5vw, 1.25rem) and 44ch.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): the base; about paragraphs are Ink Soft at 54ch. Trust strip, towns, step text, notes, footer, nav links, fine print, and bar buttons run at Body Small (0.9375rem).
- **Button** (700, 1rem, line-height 1, tabular): every button; `btn--lg` is 1.0625rem, `btn--sm` is 0.875rem.
- **Sign-off** (700, 1.05rem, -0.01em): the owner's line under the about copy, in Ink.
- **Label** (500, 0.75rem, 0.06em, uppercase, Ink Muted): the " · today" suffix in the hours table and the "Optional" mark on a field. The only tracked uppercase on the page, and it never precedes a headline.
- **Caption** (600, 0.8125rem, Ink Muted): the teaser caption ("Common jobs, posted prices."); the demo note, field errors, and concept credit reuse the size at 400.

### Named Rules
**The Heavy-Only-Loud Rule.** Public Sans 800 sets the wordmark, headlines, the band title, group titles, step titles, counters, and the hours title, always with negative tracking. It never sets a paragraph, a label, a button, or a link; 700 is the ceiling for anything read.

**The Sentence-Case Signal Rule.** The hero's context sits below the H1 as a sentence-case dispatch line, never above it as a tracked-caps eyebrow. The trust strip and the license box lead with a 700 Ink fact, not a label.

**The Tabular Rule.** Anything with a digit (prices, the dispatch line, the trust strip, the hours table, the license box, the footer, step counters, every button) sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. "from" is a 500-weight `small` at 0.85em in Ink Muted before an install price and nowhere else; flat prices carry no prefix.

## Layout

A single 1120px wrap (`min(100% - 2 * gutter, 1120px)`) with a fluid gutter (clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)) and a fluid section rhythm (clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem)). Bands alternate Paper and Paper Deep with a Line hairline at each seam; the emergency band is the one solid block, padded clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 2.75rem). The nav is sticky at 72px; `scroll-padding-top` is 84px. There is no hero photograph: the hero sits on the plain ground, padded clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5.5rem) above and clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5rem) below, and ends in a Line rule.

The hero stacks H1, dispatch line, then a grid that is one column until 1024px and 1.05fr/0.95fr above it (gap 2rem 5rem): the left column holds lede, the Call/Text pair, and the trust strip; the right holds the three-row price teaser (max 560px in one column, unbounded in two). Below 480px the hero tightens every margin (padding-top 1.75rem, H1 2.5rem, actions at 1.3rem, trust at 1.3rem, jobs at 0.5rem) so the dispatch line, both buttons, the trust strip, and all three prices sit inside one phone viewport above the fixed bar. The trust strip is a wrapping flex row (gap 0.5rem 2rem; 0.35rem 1.1rem below 480px) with `white-space: nowrap` per item, and holds to one row at every shipped width.

Breakpoints are content-specific: 480px max (the hero's compact rhythm), 600px (hero buttons stop stretching; form rows go 1fr/1fr; the form foot splits button and note), 700px (nav links appear), 760px (price groups 2-up), 900px (nav Call button appears; mobile bar hides and the body's bottom padding drops; emergency band goes auto/1fr/auto; prices head 1fr/1fr with the lede right-aligned at 40ch; how 1.1fr/0.9fr; area 1.1fr/0.9fr with the hours card sticky at 96px; about 0.9fr/1.1fr; quote 0.8fr/1.2fr; footer 1.2fr/1fr with the concept line spanning both), and 1024px (hero grid). Below 900px the body reserves `64px + safe-area-inset-bottom` for the fixed action bar (Call / Text / Quote, 1.4fr/1fr/1fr).

Photography is two Unsplash placeholders lower down: a 3:4 portrait beside the steps (max 640px tall, stretching to the column) and a 4:3 figure in the about section. Grid children carry `min-width: 0` so an image's intrinsic width cannot blow out a column; job names ellipsize rather than wrap.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from the three paper steps (page, band, white surface) and from ink hairlines; no surface, button, photo, or card carries a shadow, and photographs are cut to 6px with no veil, filter, or frame. The one elevation shadow on the page is the upward lift under the fixed mobile bar, so it reads as sitting over the scrolling page. Nav and bar are translucent chrome (70% resting, 92% stuck or fixed) with `blur(14px)`. Two zero-offset rings exist and are state, not depth: the dispatch dot's paper-and-Line-Strong halo, which separates it from the text, and the field focus halo in the accent.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Bar lift** (`box-shadow: 0 -10px 30px -18px rgba(28, 31, 38, 0.3)`): the fixed mobile bar only. Literal ink; does not follow the skin.
- **Dispatch dot ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--ground), 0 0 0 3px var(--line-strong)`): the 10px status dot, cut out from the line with a paper gap.
- **Field focus** (`0 0 0 3px rgba(var(--accent-rgb), 0.45)` with an Ink border): the only accent halo, and it is state, not elevation. `--accent-rgb` follows the skin.

### Named Rules
**The Clipboard Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest and flat on hover; hover is a 1px rise, a border change, or a fill change, never a shadow. The mobile bar is the one thing allowed to float, because it is the one thing that is not on the page.

**The Bare Photo Rule.** A photograph is cut to 6px and carries nothing: no shadow, no veil, no desaturation, no caption chrome. It is a placeholder the client replaces, and the page must not depend on it.

## Shapes

One radius. Every control, field, surface, photo, status box, license box, skip link, and the focus outline is cut at 6px (`--radius`). There are no pills: the yellow button, the outline button, the ink button, the nav button, and the mobile bar buttons are all 6px rectangles. Circles appear only as dots: 7px before trust items and towns, 8px before price rows, 10px for the dispatch status, and the scrollbar thumb. Borders are 1px hairlines in Line (surfaces, seams, rows) or Line Strong (group-title underline, resting fields, mobile bar secondary buttons); buttons carry a 1.5px border that is transparent on the accent and ink variants and Line Strong on the outline, so all three align. The band's button inverts the band's two colors. Photographs are clipped to 6px and never framed.

## Components

### Buttons
Heavy, squared-off, and one of them yellow.
- **Shape:** 6px rectangle, 1.5px border, 1rem/1.5rem padding at 1rem 700 with tabular figures and a 0.6rem gap for an inline SVG (`btn--lg`: 1.15rem/1.7rem at 1.0625rem, 1rem/1.4rem below 480px; `btn--sm`: 0.7rem/1.1rem at 0.875rem).
- **Accent:** Accent Ink on Safety Yellow. Used for "Call (970) 555-0147" in the hero (with a 20px stroke phone icon), "Call now" in the nav (from 900px), and the form submit ("Text me a quote", which reads "Sending…" while disabled). Hover to Accent Hover with a 1px rise; active presses 1px down.
- **Outline:** transparent on a Line Strong border, Ink text; hover turns the border Ink. Used for "Text us" beside the Call button (with a 20px stroke chat icon).
- **Ink:** Ink fill, Paper text, 1px rise on hover. Inside the emergency band it becomes Band Ink fill with Band text ("Call the emergency line").
- **Focus:** the global 2px Focus outline at 3px offset.
- **Text link:** inherits color, 1px underline in Line Strong at 0.2em offset; hover turns the underline currentColor. Used for Maps, phone, Text, and the VINCERE credit.

### Dots and Facts
- **Dot:** a filled circle in Safety Yellow before a row item: 7px on trust items and towns, 8px on price rows. It is the page's bullet and its only ornament.
- **Trust strip:** a Body Small flex row in Ink Soft under the hero actions, ruled above with Line, each item led by a dot with the fact in 700 Ink ("Lic. #MP-00481", "Insured to $2M", "Fort Collins + 25 miles").
- **License box:** a 6px White box on a Line border, 1rem/1.15rem, Body Small, tabular, three facts each led by a 700 term; sits under the about copy at 46ch.
- **Towns:** a two-column list at 40ch, each town a Line-ruled row with a dot, Ink Soft with the home town in 700 Ink.

### Cards / Containers
- **Hours card:** White, 1px Line, 6px radius, clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.25rem) padding, no shadow; sticky at 96px from 900px. Holds an 800 title at 1.5rem, a two-column table with Line rules and tabular figures, values right-aligned in Ink Soft, and a note in Ink Muted at 0.875rem with a 700 Ink lead. The row matching `new Date().getDay()` gets `is-today`: Ink, 700, and a " · today" suffix in the Label style.
- **Photo:** a 6px-clipped `overflow: hidden` block on a White ground with the image at `object-fit: cover`; 3:4 beside the steps, 4:3 in the about section. No treatment.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** White fill, 1px Line Strong border, 6px radius, 0.9rem/1rem padding, 3.25rem min height, inherits body type; label above at 0.875rem 700 with an optional Label-style "Optional" mark in Ink Muted.
- **Hover:** border to Ink Soft.
- **Focus:** border to Ink, 3px yellow halo at 45%, no outline.
- **Error:** Error border, message below at 0.8125rem in Error; the field also sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. The phone field wants ten digits; every other required field wants a value. Errors appear on blur once a field has content, on submit for all, and clear as the visitor types.
- **Select:** native appearance removed, an Ink Muted chevron SVG at right 1rem, placeholder option in Ink Muted.
- **Status box:** 1rem/1.25rem, Ink border on an 18% yellow wash, Ink text, 6px radius; error state swaps to an Error border. On success the form hides its rows and focuses the box. The message branches: an "emergency" answer in the When field gets "the on-call tech is calling you back in the next few minutes"; anything else gets "We'll text a flat price … within the hour".

### Navigation
- **Style:** sticky 72px bar on 70% chrome with `blur(14px) saturate(140%)`; after 24px of scroll (`is-stuck`) it deepens to 92% and gains a Line bottom rule. Wordmark left (KESTREL 800 uppercase with "Plumbing & Heating" at 500 and 60% opacity, both shown at every width), three links (Prices, Service area, About) at 0.9375rem 600 Ink Soft hovering to Ink from 700px, small accent "Call now" right from 900px.
- **Mobile action bar:** fixed to the bottom on 92% chrome with a Line top rule and the Bar lift shadow, safe-area padded, 48px 6px-radius buttons at 0.9375rem 700: Call (accent, with an 18px phone icon), Text and Quote (Ink on a 1.5px Line Strong border). Present from first paint; hidden from 900px.

### Price Rows
A job is a flex row on a Line rule at 0.8rem vertical padding: an 8px yellow dot, the name in Ink (ellipsized, with an upright Ink Muted qualifier `em` at 0.9em such as `24/7` or `credited to the repair`), and the price pushed to the right edge in Signal Text 700 tabular. Installs carry a 500-weight "from" `small` in Ink Muted; flat jobs carry nothing. The same row serves the hero teaser (three rows under a 0.8125rem 600 caption) and the four price groups (800 title over a Line Strong rule, 2-up from 760px). The note runs at 0.9375rem Ink Muted, 62ch, with a 600 Ink lead.

### Steps
An ordered list capped at 52ch on the Paper Deep band: each step is a 2.5rem/1fr grid on a Line rule with an 800 counter (`01` to `04`) in Signal Text at 1.35rem spanning both rows, an 800 title at 1.25rem, and Body Small text in Ink Soft.

### Emergency Band (signature)
The one solid block on the page: Band ground, Band Ink text, padded clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 2.75rem), stacked until 900px and auto/1fr/auto above it. An 800 title at clamp(1.4rem, 2.6vw, 2rem) ("Water where it shouldn't be?"), a 1rem text at 90% opacity and 52ch with the after-hours rate in 700, and the inverted ink button ("Call the emergency line"). It sits directly under the hero, before prices, so the rate is stated plainly before any pitch.

### Dispatch Line and Call/Text Pair (signature)
`kestrel.js` holds the `HOURS` table (JS weekday keys, 24h decimal ranges, `null` for Sunday) and a four-truck `TRUCKS` board of booked `[start, end]` blocks, and derives the hero line from `new Date()`: the earliest free half-hour slot across the four trucks at or after now, never inside the last hour before close (`LAST_DISPATCH_BEFORE_CLOSE`). The line reads "Open now · next truck 2:30 pm" (or "a truck can roll now" inside the next half hour, or "trucks booked today, emergency line open"), "Opens at 7 am · first truck 7 am · emergency line open" before opening, or "After hours · emergency line open · next scheduled truck tomorrow 7 am" (or the weekday) otherwise, always followed by " · Fort Collins". It is Signal Text 600 tabular at 1.0625rem with a 10px yellow dot ringed in paper and Line Strong; when closed the dot turns Ink Muted. It sits under the H1 with `aria-live="polite"`, and before script runs it reads "Monday to Saturday from 7 am · Fort Collins". Directly under it, the two thumb-width actions: the yellow "Call (970) 555-0147" (`tel:`) and the outlined "Text us" (`sms:`), stretching full width below 600px and sitting side by side above it. The same `HOURS` table marks today's row in the hours card; the HTML table is the visible copy and the JS table is the copy the page reasons from. Keep both in sync.

### Motion
One easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and three durations (160ms, 320ms, 700ms), gated behind a `.js` class so nothing is hidden without script. The hero title, dispatch line, lede, actions, trust strip, and teaser rise 14px over 700ms with an 80ms stagger (0/80/160/240/320/400ms). There is no hero drift because there is no hero photograph. The band, prices head and groups, how copy and photo, area copy, hours card, about media and copy, quote copy, and form rise 12px over 700ms once, on intersection (8% threshold, -8% bottom margin), staggered 60ms in groups of four. Button hover and field borders run at 160ms; nav chrome and the skip link at 320ms. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes every animation, collapses transitions, disables smooth scroll, and skips the reveal observer.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every color in the section 1 token block and add a client's brand as a new `html[data-skin="<id>"]` block that redefines all of them, including `--accent-text`, `--band`, and `--band-ink`; on a light ground set `--accent-text` to a dark value.
- **Do** keep Public Sans 800 with negative tracking for the wordmark, headlines, titles, and counters only, and keep 700 as the ceiling for anything read.
- **Do** keep the hero's context below the H1 as a sentence-case dispatch line, and lead facts with a 700 Ink term rather than a label.
- **Do** rule with ink hairlines (16% and 32%) and section with the three paper steps; cut everything at 6px; point with a 7px or 8px yellow dot.
- **Do** set tabular numerals on every number and put "from" only on install prices.
- **Do** keep every surface and photograph flat; the mobile bar's lift is the only shadow.
- **Do** keep Call, Text, and Quote in the fixed bar below 900px and the accent "Call now" in the nav above it, and keep the dispatch line, Call/Text, trust strip, and three prices inside one phone viewport above the bar.
- **Do** drive hours from the one `HOURS` table and the `TRUCKS` board: dispatch line and today's row.
- **Do** cap running copy at 40 to 62ch and headings at 16ch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set the yellow as text on a light ground, use it as a running ground outside the emergency band, or add a second accent; don't use `--accent` where `--accent-text` is meant, or the reverse.
- **Don't** put a tracked-caps eyebrow above the H1 or an H2, and don't set Public Sans 800 at body sizes or in a paragraph.
- **Don't** add pills, radii other than 6px, borders in the accent, or a shadow on any surface, button, card, or photograph.
- **Don't** veil, desaturate, or frame a photograph, and don't add a hero photograph: the hero paints on the plain ground.
- **Don't** add van slideshows, "call for a free estimate", badge walls, ratings, reviews, or any proof device; Kestrel is fictional and the page must not fabricate.
- **Don't** write skin-specific selectors below the token block except `color-scheme` and the Forest band button; if a skin needs one, it is a new base.
- **Don't** index this page: keep `noindex, nofollow` and keep it out of `sitemap.xml`.

## Reusing this base for a client

Kestrel is the base; a client site is a copy of `concepts/kestrel/` with these replaced.

- **Tokens:** either overwrite the `:root` block in `kestrel.css` (the client becomes the default) or add `html[data-skin="<client>"]` with all sixteen color tokens (accent, accent-text, accent-rgb, accent-ink, accent-hover, band, band-ink, and the neutrals) and `--chrome`, and set the attribute in the `<head>` script (or accept it via `?skin=`). Check Ink Soft and Ink Muted contrast on the new ground; if the accent cannot clear AA as text, set `--accent-text` to ink as Safety does.
- **Photography:** the two images are Unsplash placeholders (`images.unsplash.com`, with `srcset`). Replace with the client's rasters at the same aspect ratios (3:4 beside the steps, 4:3 in about), keep them bare, and write real `alt` text for the about figure.
- **Hours and trucks:** edit `HOURS` in `kestrel.js` (keys 0 to 6, `[open, close]` in 24h decimal, `null` for closed), `LAST_DISPATCH_BEFORE_CLOSE`, `TOWN`, and the visible `<table data-hours>` rows, the footer hours line, and the JSON-LD `openingHoursSpecification`. Replace the `TRUCKS` demonstration board with the dispatch software's real availability, or the dispatch line is fiction.
- **License, phone, address:** the `tel:` and `sms:` links (nav, hero, band, quote lede, footer, bar), the trust strip, the license box, the footer line, the Maps `destination`, and the JSON-LD `telephone`, `address`, and `areaServed`.
- **Copy:** `<title>`, `meta description`, the H1 and lede, the band, the price groups and prices, the steps, the towns, the about story and sign-off, the quote fine print, the `issue` and `when` options, and the wordmark text in the nav and footer.
- **Form endpoint:** replace `send()` in `kestrel.js` with a POST to the dispatch software (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro) or the on-call phone, remove the "Concept form" note, and keep the validation and the emergency branch.
- **Meta:** replace `theme-color` with the client's ground, remove `noindex` and the footer's fictional-business line, add the page to the sitemap, and update `color-scheme` if the default skin is dark.
