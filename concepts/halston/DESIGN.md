# Design System: Halston & Co.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Button and the Board Behind It"**

Halston & Co. is a one-page barber-shop site that is one action ("Book a chair") and everything a man on his phone needs before pressing it: whether he can get in, with whom, what it costs, and when the door is open, each within one scroll. The first screen carries the name, a three-line headline the size of the screen ("THE / CLASSIC / CUT."), two chips ("Walk-ins welcome", "Hot towel shave"), one sentence, the filled BOOK A CHAIR beside an outline CALL, and along the foot of the hero a hairline hours bar whose middle is live ("Open · closes 7 pm · next chair 3:40 pm"). Below it the services are a price ledger with dotted leaders and one price each, the four barbers are tracked monograms rather than portraits, the hours and address sit in the middle for the visitor who only wanted to check them, and the form arrives with the barber and the service already chosen. Remove the copy and you would still recognize it: black, one off-white block button, uppercase tracked hairline labels, a headline the size of the screen.

It is VINCERE's sixth template base ("one structure, many skins") and the sibling of Sasso, Apex, Kestrel, Auburn, and Northline. Every color on the page lives in one token block at the top of `halston.css`; a skin is a second, complete token block on `html[data-skin]` and nothing structural changes beneath it. Two alternate skins ship (Ivory, Oxblood) and match the three-way switcher in the VINCERE home page's concept panel. Unlike the siblings, there is no accent hue: the ink is the action. `--accent` equals `--ink` on Mono and Ivory (Oxblood alone tints it to a pale rose), so the filled button is the page's own text color as a block and there is no second hue anywhere. The `--chrome` triplet equals the ground so the nav and the mobile bar are the page at 70%, 92%, and 94%. The ground is not one value but a settle: the body is a gradient from `--ground` (held to 40%) into `--ground-deep` by the foot, which is the only gradient on the page and the only thing that could be mistaken for a band fill. Halston is fictional, carries `noindex, nofollow`, and is never listed in the sitemap; its palette and type are deliberately not VINCERE tokens (see the root DESIGN.md's "Concept Previews" exception).

The type is the argument: the card sells "a type-led layout that needs no photography to feel finished", and the page proves it with Archivo alone, loaded at 400, 500, 700, and 800 from Google Fonts. 800 uppercase at -0.04em for the H1 and every H2; 500 tracked uppercase at 0.10 to 0.16em for every label, nav link, and chip; 700 for names, prices, and buttons; 400 for the little running text there is, at 1.0625rem. Density is tight: sections sit on a clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem) rhythm, copy is capped at 30 to 60ch, and there is not one card on the page; hairlines rule the rows, the barbers, the hours, and the seams. Nothing is lifted, nothing is photographed, nothing is rounded past 2px. Confirmed rejections (from the direction contract and held in the build): a moody photograph of a straight razor, an Instagram embed in place of a menu, "prices vary" or "call for details", a booking link that opens someone else's app, reviews, ratings, "voted best", and an eyebrow kicker above the headline.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two blacks (#101012 settling to #08080A) do the sectioning as one gradient; hairlines at 18% and 38% ink do the ruling; there are no band fills and no cards
- One color for the action and it is the ink: #F2F2F0 fills BOOK, is the caret, the selection, the focus ring, the hours-bar dot, and the sent-form border; Oxblood is the only skin where `--accent` and `--ink` differ
- Archivo 800 uppercase at -0.04em for the H1 (0.92) and every H2 (0.95); 500 tracked uppercase at 0.14em for labels; 700 for names, prices, and buttons; 400 at 1.0625rem for reading; nothing bolder than 800, nothing at 600
- One radius: 2px on every button, chip, field, monogram, status box, and focus outline; circles only as the 7px hours-bar dot and the scrollbar thumb
- Flat and dry: no shadow anywhere, not even under the fixed mobile bar; chrome is the ground at 70/92/94% with a 14px blur
- Tabular numerals on every price, every time, the hours bar, the hours table, the barber roles, and the footer hours
- Dotted leaders on the ledger (1px dotted Line Strong), a tracked 4.5rem monogram in a hairline box for each barber, and a hairline hours bar whose middle is live
- A skin is a token block on `html[data-skin]`, selected by `?skin=<id>`, and the `<head>` script rewrites `theme-color` and `color-scheme` to match; below the token block only `color-scheme` and the select chevron are skin-scoped
- One easing (out-expo) and three durations (160/320/600ms); the three headline lines rise once with a 60ms stagger and nothing else on the page animates but hovers

## Colors

A monochrome palette where two near-blacks carry structure as a single settle, off-white ink carries every word and number and is also the only fill, and hairlines at two opacities of that ink do all the ruling.

### Primary
- **Ink as Action** (`{colors.accent}`): the one fill. Fills BOOK in the nav, BOOK A CHAIR in the hero, the section foot, the hours card, the form submit ("Book it", which reads "Sending…" while disabled), and the mobile bar; fills a ledger row's "Book" on hover; is `accent-color`, `caret-color`, and text selection. Equal to Ink on Mono and Ivory; a pale rose (#E9CFC3) on Oxblood so the block reads warmer than the text.
- **Ink on Action** (`{colors.accent-ink}`): text on the filled button, the deep ground (#08080A), so the block is a hole cut in the page rather than a button sitting on it. Ivory inverts it to the ivory ground; Oxblood to the deep oxblood.
- **Action Hover** (`{colors.accent-hover}`): the filled button on hover with a 1px rise: pure white on Mono, pure black on Ivory, the ink on Oxblood.
- **Focus** (`{colors.focus}`): the global 2px focus outline at 3px offset, cut at 2px. Equal to the accent on every skin.

### Neutral
- **Black** (`{colors.ground}`): the page ground at the top, the `theme-color`, and the source of the `--chrome` triplet (16, 16, 18) that builds the nav and the mobile bar; also the text on the skip link. Not pure black: a whisper of blue.
- **Black Deep** (`{colors.ground-deep}`): the `<html>` ground, the scrollbar thumb's border, and what the body gradient settles into from 40% to 100% of the page. Also the text on the filled button.
- **Off-White** (`{colors.ink}`): the H1, every H2, names, prices, the wordmark, chips, the hours-bar live line and its dot when open, the barber days, field text, today's row in the hours table, outline buttons, the ledger "Book", and the sent-form status box's border and text. Warm, not blue-white.
- **Ink Soft** (`{colors.ink-soft}`): the hero sentence, ledes, nav links at rest, the hours bar's two fixed cells and the live line when closed, barber text, the hours table at rest, facts' definitions, field labels, the footer, the select chevron, and the field hover border. Marked 8.4:1 on Black.
- **Ink Muted** (`{colors.ink-muted}`): ledger notes, barber roles, facts' terms, the hours note, fine print, placeholders, the unselected select, the " · today" suffix, the demo note, the concept credit, and the hours-bar dot when closed. Marked 5.9:1 on Black.
- **Line** and **Line Strong** (`{colors.line}`, `{colors.line-strong}`): 18% and 38% ink. Line rules ledger rows, hours rows, the hours bar's top, the barbers' and book's section seams, the stuck nav, the bar's top, the footer, and the footer's concept seam; Line Strong tops the ledger, each barber, and the hours block, draws the dotted leaders, boxes the monograms, and borders every chip, field, outline button, ledger "Book", and bar Call at rest, and colors the scrollbar thumb.
- **Error** (`{colors.error}`): a salmon for invalid field borders, error messages, and the status box's error border; a brick red on Ivory, a paler salmon on Oxblood.

### Skins
A skin is a second token block on `html[data-skin="<id>"]` that redefines the same names. `?skin=ivory` or `?skin=oxblood` on the URL sets the attribute from an inline script in `<head>`, before first paint, and rewrites `theme-color` to the skin's ground and `color-scheme` to its polarity, so the home page's "Open the full concept" link opens the look the visitor chose in the switcher with the browser chrome to match. The chrome triplet always equals the skin's ground.

- **Ivory** (`{colors.ivory-*}`): warm off-white grounds (#F4F0E8 settling to #EAE4D8), near-black ink (#121212) that is also the fill with the ivory ground on it; hover pure black. Hairlines at 16% and 36%; `color-scheme` flips to light; the error hardens to brick. The only light skin. Ink Soft 9.0:1, Ink Muted 5.3:1.
- **Oxblood** (`{colors.oxblood-*}`): deep oxblood grounds (#431719 settling to #2C0F11), rose-white ink (#F6E9E3), and the one skin where the fill is not the ink: a pale rose (#E9CFC3) with the deep oxblood on it, hovering to the ink. Hairlines at 20% and 40%, the highest of the three; error a pale salmon; dark. Ink Soft 7.4:1, Ink Muted 5.5:1.

### Named Rules
**The Ink Is the Action Rule.** There is no accent hue. The filled button is `--accent` with `--accent-ink` on it, and on Mono and Ivory `--accent` equals `--ink`: the action is the text color as a block. A skin may warm the fill (Oxblood) but may not introduce a second hue; anything on the page that is not text and not a hairline is the ink as a block, and there are exactly six of them plus the bar.

**The Settle Rule.** The ground is one gradient, from `--ground` (held to 40%) into `--ground-deep` by the foot, on `<body>`; sections carry no fill of their own and are cut apart by Line hairlines. There are no band fills, no tinted sections, and no other gradient on the page.

**The Hairline Rule.** Rules are 1px of ink at 18% (Line) or 38% (Line Strong), and the ledger's leaders are 1px dotted Line Strong. Line Strong tops what begins (the ledger, a barber, the hours block) and borders what is pressable at rest; Line rules what repeats (rows, seams) and tops the chrome. There is no solid divider color and no border in the accent except a hovered ledger "Book" and the sent-form status box (which borders in Ink).

**The Chrome Is the Ground Rule.** `--chrome` is the ground as an RGB triplet, never a separate tint. The nav is the page at 70% (92% once stuck, when it gains its Line rule) and the mobile bar is the page at 94%, both with `blur(14px)`. A skin that changes its ground changes the chrome.

**The Token Block Rule.** Every color on the page resolves to a token in section 1 of `halston.css`. A skin redefines that block and nothing else; the only skin-scoped selectors below it are `color-scheme` and the select chevron's stroke (one data-URI per skin, each in that skin's Ink Soft). A brand that needs a structural override is a new base, not a skin.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial)
**Body Font:** Archivo (same stack)

One family, loaded from Google Fonts at 400, 500, 700, 800 only.

**Character:** A grotesque set the way a barber's window is lettered: big, capital, tight, and dry. At 800 uppercase with -0.04em tracking and a line-height under 1 the headline is a block of black-and-white, not a sentence; the same setting at section size makes every H2 a sign, each ending in a period at 40% opacity. At 500 uppercase with 0.10 to 0.16em of tracking the family does the labeling: nav, hours bar, roles, days, field labels, chips. At 700 it names and prices; at 400 it says the one thing that needs saying and stops.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(4.25rem, 19vw, 10rem), 0.92, -0.04em, uppercase): the hero H1 only ("The / Classic / Cut."), three explicit lines, spanning the wrap.
- **Display Wide** (800, clamp(6rem, 12.5vw, 11.25rem), 0.92, -0.04em, uppercase): the same H1 from 900px, where the side column tucks into the room right of "CUT.".
- **Headline** (800, clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 4.5rem), 0.95, -0.04em, uppercase): section H2s ("Services.", "The chairs.", "Find us.", "Book a chair."), each ending in a period at 40% opacity. Sentence case in the markup, uppercase by CSS.
- **Hours Title** (800, 1.5rem, -0.03em, uppercase): "Hours" in the hours block.
- **Monogram** (800, 1.375rem, 0.08em, uppercase): the two initials in each barber's 4.5rem box, with `text-indent` matching the tracking so they center.
- **Title** (700, 1.25rem, 1.15, -0.02em): the four barber names.
- **Ledger** (700, 1.125rem, -0.01em): a service's name and, tabular, its price at the right edge.
- **Wordmark** (700, 0.9375rem, 0.16em, uppercase): "Halston & Co." in the nav and the footer. Shown at every width.
- **Hero Sub** (400, clamp(1.0625rem, 1.5vw, 1.25rem), 1.5, Ink Soft): "Four chairs, no gimmicks. Cuts from $38, in and out in forty minutes.", capped at 30ch.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(1.0625rem, 1.4vw, 1.2rem), 1.5): the paragraph beside each H2, Ink Soft, capped at 40ch, right-aligned from 800px.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.55): the base; facts' definitions, the footer's address, field text (at 1rem), the status box.
- **Body Small** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.5): ledger notes and barber text (Ink Soft), fine print and the hours note (Ink Muted, 48ch), the footer.
- **Caption** (400, 0.875rem, 1.5): the demo note and the concept credit in Ink Muted, field errors in Error. The floor for running copy.
- **Button** (700, 0.875rem, line-height 1, 0.12em, uppercase): every `.btn`; `btn--sm` and the ledger "Book" at 0.75rem; the mobile bar at 0.8125rem; the skip link at 0.8125rem with 0.1em.

The page's labeling voice; it sits beside or below what it labels, never above a headline.

### Named Rules
**The Capital Rule.** Headlines are 800 uppercase at -0.04em with a line-height under 1 (0.92 for the H1, 0.95 for H2s); labels are 500 uppercase tracked 0.10 to 0.16em; buttons are 700 uppercase tracked 0.12em. Everything read (the hero sentence, ledes, notes, barber text, field text) is 400 sentence case with no tracking. A weight of 600 does not exist on the page, and nothing is uppercase without tracking.

**The Below-Not-Above Rule.** The hero's context is the hours bar along the foot of the hero and the chips beside the sentence, never a kicker above the H1. An H2 is followed by its lede, never preceded by a label. Tracked uppercase labels are this world's native voice for naming a thing (a field, a fact, a role), and they sit with the thing they name.

**The Tabular Rule.** Anything with a digit that lines up (ledger prices, the hours bar, the hours table, the footer hours, barber roles with their years) sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Prices are one number in Ink 700 with no prefix and no "from"; every service on the page has a price.

## Layout

A single 1120px wrap (`min(100% - 2 * gutter, 1120px)`) with a fluid gutter (clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)) and a fluid section rhythm (clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem)), the tightest of the six bases. Sections carry no fills; the barbers section is ruled top and bottom with Line, the book section on top, and the footer on top. The nav is sticky at 72px on 70% chrome; `scroll-padding-top` is 80px. The hero pulls up under the nav (`margin-top: -72px`, `padding-top: 72px`), is `min(100svh, 920px)` tall (`min(100svh - 64px, 920px)` below 700px so the live line clears the fixed bar), and is a two-row grid: the inner content centered in the first row, the hours bar in the second.

The hero's inner grid stacks the H1 and the side column (chips, sentence, actions) at a 2.5rem gap, padded clamp(3rem, 8vw, 6rem) above and clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3.5rem) below. From 900px both take `grid-area: 1 / 1`: the headline spans the wrap at Display Wide and the side column (a 21rem block) sits at the end, aligned to the bottom, in the room to the right of "CUT.". Section heads are a grid of H2 and lede at 1.25rem, going `auto 1fr` from 800px with the lede right-aligned at the end. The hours bar is a Line rule with 1.1rem/1.25rem of padding and one Label line; from 700px it is `1fr auto 1fr` (fixed hours left, live status center, address right) and below it the cells stack with the live line first.

Breakpoints are content-specific: 600px (below it a ledger row goes 2-column with the price beside the name and "Book" under the note; from it the crew goes 2-up, form rows 1fr/1fr, and the form foot splits button and note auto/1fr), 700px (hours bar 3-column), 760px (nav links appear), 800px (section heads split), 900px (nav BOOK and the hero actions appear, the H1 goes wide and the side column tucks in, visit 1.1fr/0.9fr with the hours block sticky at 96px, book 0.8fr/1.2fr, footer 1.2fr/1fr with the concept line spanning both, the mobile bar hides, the body's bottom padding drops, and the section-level "Book a chair" links show), 1000px (crew 4-up). Below 900px the body reserves `64px + safe-area-inset-bottom` for the fixed bar (Book a chair / Call, 1.6fr/1fr).

No rasters ship: the page has no photography, no icons beyond the select chevron, and no provenance to carry. Barbers are monograms; the shop is its name. Grid children carry `min-width: 0` so a long word cannot blow out a column; ledger names never wrap (the leader takes the slack) and ledger notes do.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat and dry. Depth comes from the ground settling from Black to Black Deep down the page, from the filled button reading as a hole cut in the ink, and from hairlines at two opacities; nothing casts a shadow, not a button, a field, a monogram, the nav, or the fixed mobile bar (the siblings' one exception is absent here; the bar separates by its Line rule and its 94% chrome). Nav and bar are translucent chrome in the ground triplet with `blur(14px)`. Hover is a 1px rise, a border change, or a fill change. There are no rings: field focus is a border change to Ink and the global focus outline is a 2px Ink line at 3px offset.

### Shadow Vocabulary
None. `box-shadow` does not appear in `halston.css`.

- **Focus ring** (`outline: 2px solid var(--focus); outline-offset: 3px; border-radius: 2px`): the global keyboard ring in Ink (the rose on Oxblood). State, not depth.
- **Chrome** (`background: rgba(var(--chrome), 0.7); backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`): the nav at rest; 0.92 when stuck; 0.94 on the fixed bar. The triplet equals the ground.

### Named Rules
**The No-Shadow Rule.** Nothing on the page is lifted, including the fixed bar. Surfaces are flat at rest and flat on hover; hover is a 1px rise, a border to Ink, or a fill to the accent, never a shadow, never a glow. A future surface that needs separation from scrolling content gets a Line rule and denser chrome, not a cast.

**The No-Picture Rule.** The page ships no rasters and no illustration: no hero photograph, no portraits, no product shots, no razor. A barber is a two-letter monogram in a hairline box; a service is a name, a note, and a price. A client's photographs, if any, are a new base's problem, not this one's.

## Shapes

One radius and a circle. Every control is cut at 2px (`--radius`): the filled and outline buttons, the small buttons, the ledger "Book", the chips, the monogram boxes, inputs and selects, the sent-form status box, the skip link, the bar's two buttons, and the focus outline. Circles appear only as the 7px dot before the hours-bar status and the scrollbar thumb. Borders are 1px hairlines: Line Strong on every chip, field, outline button, ledger "Book", monogram, and bar Call at rest, and on top of the ledger, each barber, and the hours block; Line on rows, seams, chrome, and the hours bar's top. The filled button carries a 1px border in its own fill so it aligns with the outline beside it. The ledger's leader is a 1px dotted Line Strong line, flexed to fill the gap between name and price and raised 0.3em to sit at the baseline. Nothing is clipped, nothing is masked, nothing is a pill.

## Components

### Buttons
Square, capital, tracked, and one of them the ink as a block.
- **Shape:** 2px corners, 1px border, 1.05rem/1.6rem padding at 0.875rem 700 uppercase 0.12em with a 0.6rem gap (`btn--sm`: 0.8rem/1.1rem at 0.75rem). Labels are wrapped in a `<span>` set `nowrap`.
- **Fill:** Ink on Action on Ink as Action, border the same. Used for BOOK in the nav (from 900px), BOOK A CHAIR in the hero (from 900px), the services foot and the hours block (from 900px), the form submit ("Book it" / "Sending…"), and the mobile bar. Hover to Action Hover with a 1px rise; active presses 1px down.
- **Outline:** transparent on a Line Strong border, Ink text; hover turns the border Ink. Used for CALL in the hero and, small, for the four "Book with <name>" buttons.
- **Focus:** the global 2px Focus outline at 3px offset.
- **Ledger Book:** the small square on every service row: 0.5rem/0.75rem at 0.75rem 700 uppercase 0.12em, Ink on a Line Strong border, raised 0.3rem to sit on the name's line (dropped below 600px). Hover fills it with the accent. The only accent border on the page besides the sent status box.
- **Text link:** inherits color, 1px underline in Line Strong at 0.2em offset; hover turns the underline currentColor. Used for "Open in Maps", the phone number (visit and footer), and the VINCERE credit.

### Chips
- **Style:** 2px corners, transparent, 1px Line Strong border, 0.5rem/0.75rem, 0.6875rem 500 uppercase 0.1em in Ink, `nowrap`.
- **State:** static; two facts a man wants before he scrolls ("Walk-ins welcome", "Hot towel shave"), in the hero's side column under an aria-label of "Good to know". Not filters, not tags.

### Ledger (signature)
- **Style:** an `<ol>` topped by Line Strong; each row a grid of name / note / price / Book on a Line rule at 1.1rem vertical padding, columns `minmax(0, 1fr) auto auto`. The name is Ledger (700, -0.01em, `nowrap`) followed by a dotted leader that flexes to the price; the note (0.9375rem Ink Muted: what the service is and how long it takes) sits under the name; the price is Ledger tabular at the right edge with no prefix; the "Book" square sits after it. Below 600px the grid goes `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` with "Book" under the note.
- **Content:** eight services, every one priced ($15 to $75) with a duration. Each "Book" carries `data-book-service`; tapping it sets the Service select, scrolls to the form, and focuses Barber (or Day if a barber is already chosen).

### Barbers (signature)
- **Style:** a `<ul>` of four `<li>` at gap clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem) / clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem), 1-up, 2-up from 600px, 4-up from 1000px. Each is a Line Strong top rule, 1.25rem of air, and six grid rows (monogram, name, role, text, days, Book) with the text row taking the slack so the four "Book with" buttons sit on one line.
- **Monogram:** a 4.5rem square at 2px on a Line Strong border with two initials at Monogram (800, 1.375rem, 0.08em), `aria-hidden`. The portrait this world does not take.
- **Text:** the name at Title, the role at Label in Ink Muted ("Owner · Chair 1 · 19 years", tabular), 0.9375rem Ink Soft copy, the days at Label in Ink, and a small outline "Book with <first name>" carrying `data-book-barber`.

### Hours Block
- **Style:** not a card: a Line Strong top rule and 1.25rem of air, then Hours Title, a two-column table at Body with Line rules and tabular figures (day left in Ink Soft, hours right, `nowrap`), a note at 0.9375rem Ink Muted, and a filled "Book a chair" (desktop only). Sticky at 96px from 900px.
- **Today:** the row matching `new Date().getDay()` gets `is-today`: Ink 700 with a " · today" suffix at Chip size in Ink Muted.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** transparent fill, 1px Line Strong border, 2px corners, 0.95rem/1rem padding, 3.375rem min height, 1rem text in Ink; label above at Label in Ink Soft. Placeholders in Ink Muted.
- **Hover:** border to Ink Soft.
- **Focus:** border to Ink, no outline, no halo.
- **Error:** Error border, message below at 0.875rem in Error; the field also sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. The phone field wants ten digits; the day must be an open day; every other required field wants a value. Errors appear on blur once a field has content, on submit for all, and clear as the visitor types or changes a select.
- **Select:** native appearance removed, a 16px chevron SVG at right 1rem in the skin's Ink Soft (three data-URIs, one per skin), placeholder option in Ink Muted. Barber is the four names plus "First available"; Service is the eight ledger rows; Day is a select of the next ten open days filled by `halston.js` ("Today", "Tomorrow", then "Wed Sep 16"), so a Sunday cannot be chosen and today drops out after the last chair at 6:30; Time is four bands (Morning 9–12, Midday 12–3, Afternoon 3–5, Evening 5–7).
- **Status box:** 1.1rem/1.25rem, Ink border, 2px, Ink text at Body; error state swaps to an Error border. On success the form hides its rows and foot, and the box takes focus.

### Navigation
- **Style:** sticky 72px bar on 70% chrome with `blur(14px)`; after 24px of scroll (`is-stuck`) it deepens to 92% and gains a Line bottom rule. Wordmark left ("Halston & Co.", 700, 0.16em, uppercase, shown at every width), four links (Services, Barbers, Hours, Visit) at Label in Ink Soft hovering to Ink from 760px, small filled BOOK right from 900px.
- **Mobile action bar:** fixed to the bottom on 94% chrome with a Line top rule and no shadow, safe-area padded, two 50px squares at 0.8125rem 700 uppercase 0.12em: Book a chair (filled) at 1.6fr and Call (Ink on a Line Strong border) at 1fr. Present from first paint; hidden from 900px. Under 900px it is the one repeat of Book/Call: the hero pair, the nav BOOK, and the section-level "Book a chair" links are desktop-only, while the ledger "Book" and "Book with" buttons stay at every width because they carry data into the form.

### The Hours Bar (signature)
The foot of the hero: a Line rule, then one Label line in Ink Soft with tabular figures, `1fr auto 1fr` from 700px. Left "Mon–Sat · 9–7", right "412 Canal St", and in the middle the live line in Ink with a 7px Ink dot before it. `halston.js` holds the `HOURS` table (JS weekday keys, 24h decimal ranges, `null` for Sunday), `LAST_CHAIR` (18.5), and `OPENINGS` (a fictional week of chair start times per weekday), and derives the line from `new Date()`: "Open · closes 7 pm · next chair 3:40 pm", "Opens 9 am · next chair 9:30 am" before opening, or "Closed · opens tomorrow 9 am · next chair tomorrow 9 am" (or the weekday) otherwise; `nextChair` walks up to eight days and skips anything within 15 minutes of now on the first day. When closed the line and its dot drop to Ink Soft and Ink Muted. It carries `aria-live="polite"`, and before script runs it reads "Canal Street". Below 700px it stacks with the live line first, and the hero shortens by the bar's height so the line stays above the fold. The same table marks today's row in the hours block and fills the Day select; the HTML table is the visible copy and the JS table is the copy the page reasons from. Keep both in sync.

### Motion
One easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and three durations (160ms, 320ms, 600ms), gated behind a `.js` class so nothing is hidden without script. The three headline lines rise 12px over 600ms with a 60ms stagger (0/60/120ms). Nothing else animates: there is no reveal observer, no drift, and the sections below the hero are static. Button fills, borders, link underlines, nav link color, and field borders run at 160ms; nav chrome and the skip link at 320ms. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes every animation, collapses transitions, disables smooth scroll, and makes the ledger's and barbers' scroll-to-form and focus instant.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every color in the section 1 token block and add a client's brand as a new `html[data-skin="<id>"]` block that redefines all of them, including `--accent`, `--accent-ink`, `--accent-hover`, `--chrome`, `--error`, and `--focus`; keep `--accent` equal to `--ink` unless the skin warms it, set `--chrome` to the ground, add the skin to the `<head>` script's chrome map, and add a select chevron in the skin's Ink Soft.
- **Do** keep Archivo 800 uppercase at -0.04em with a line-height under 1 for the H1 and H2s only; 500 tracked uppercase at 0.10 to 0.16em for labels, nav links, and chips; 700 for names, prices, and buttons; 400 sentence case at 1.0625rem for reading; weights 400, 500, 700, 800 and nothing at 600.
- **Do** keep the hero's context along its foot as the hours bar and beside the sentence as chips; follow an H2 with its lede and end it with a period at 40% opacity.
- **Do** rule with ink hairlines (18% and 38%) and dotted leaders; cut every control at 2px; top what begins with Line Strong and rule what repeats with Line; let the ground settle from Black to Black Deep as the page's one gradient.
- **Do** set tabular numerals on prices, the hours bar, the hours table, barber roles, and the footer hours; price every service as one number with a duration and no prefix.
- **Do** keep the page flat with no shadow anywhere, including the fixed bar, and keep it free of rasters: a barber is a monogram in a hairline box.
- **Do** keep Book a chair and Call in the fixed bar below 900px as the one repeat, and the filled BOOK in the nav, the hero pair, and the section-level "Book a chair" links above it; keep the ledger "Book" and "Book with" buttons at every width.
- **Do** drive the hours bar, today's row, and the Day select from the one `HOURS` table and the next chair from the one `OPENINGS` table.
- **Do** carry `data-book-service` on every ledger row and `data-book-barber` on every barber so the form always arrives prefilled, and keep the Service select's options identical to the ledger.
- **Do** cap running copy at 30 to 48ch (the concept line at 60ch), ledes at 40ch, and the hero sentence at 30ch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second hue, a colored accent, a tinted section, or a band fill; don't set the filled button in anything but `--accent` with `--accent-ink` on it.
- **Don't** put a kicker above the H1 or a label above an H2, set a headline in anything but 800 uppercase, or use a weight of 600 anywhere.
- **Don't** add a radius other than 2px (the 7px dot and the scrollbar thumb are the only circles), a pill, a card, a border in the accent outside a hovered ledger "Book" and the sent status box, or a shadow on anything, including the bar.
- **Don't** add a photograph, a portrait, an illustration, or an icon beyond the select chevron; don't replace a monogram with a face.
- **Don't** add a native date input, "prices vary", "call for details", a booking link into someone else's app, an Instagram embed, ratings, reviews, "voted best", or any proof device; Halston is fictional and the page must not fabricate.
- **Don't** write skin-specific selectors below the token block except `color-scheme` and the select chevron; if a skin needs one, it is a new base.
- **Don't** index this page: keep `noindex, nofollow` and keep it out of `sitemap.xml`.

## Reusing this base for a client

Halston is the base; a client site is a copy of `concepts/halston/` with these replaced.

- **Tokens:** either overwrite the `:root` block in `halston.css` (the client becomes the default) or add `html[data-skin="<client>"]` with all of the color tokens and `--chrome`, add the skin to the `<head>` script's regex and chrome map (ground hex and `light`/`dark`), and add a `.field select` chevron data-URI in the skin's Ink Soft. Check Ink Soft and Ink Muted contrast on the new ground; the marks in the CSS comments are the values to beat.
- **Photography:** none ships and none is owed; there is no provenance to carry. If a client insists on portraits, that is a new base, not a skin: the monogram grid and the hero's type-only composition are the point of this one.
- **Hours and openings:** edit `HOURS` in `halston.js` (keys 0 to 6, `[open, close]` in 24h decimal, `null` for closed), `LAST_CHAIR`, the visible `<table data-hours>` rows, the hours bar's fixed cells, the footer hours line, and the hours note; replace `OPENINGS` with a feed from the shop's book (Squire, Booksy, or a calendar) so the next chair is real.
- **Services:** the eight ledger rows (name, note with duration, price, `data-book-service`) and the Service select's options, kept identical.
- **Barbers:** the four monograms, names, roles, copy, days, and `data-book-barber` values, and the Barber select's options plus "First available".
- **Phone, address:** the `tel:` links (hero, visit, footer, bar), the address in the hours bar, visit, and footer, the Maps `destination`, and the parking and walk-in facts.
- **Copy:** `<title>`, `meta description`, the three H1 lines (keep them three; the layout and the rise are built for it), the two chips, the hero sentence, the four H2s and ledes, and the wordmark text in the nav and footer.
- **Form endpoint:** replace `send()` in `halston.js` with a POST to the shop's book, remove the "Concept form" note, and keep the validation, the open-days select, the prefill from ledger and barbers, and the confirmation that names the barber.
- **Meta:** replace `theme-color` with the client's ground, remove `noindex` and the footer's fictional-shop line, add the page to the sitemap, and update the `color-scheme` meta if the default skin is light.
