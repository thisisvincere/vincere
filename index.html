    How does a project work?

    Every project starts with a short conversation about your business, your customers, and what the site needs to do. From there we map the structure, design the pages, build them, and launch. You see the work as it develops: no long silences, no surprises at the end.

    How long does a website take?

    It depends on scope. A focused marketing site is usually a matter of weeks rather than months. Larger sites with more pages, integrations, or custom functionality take longer. You get a timeline together with your quote, before any work starts.

    How much does a website cost?

    Every project is different. After learning about your business and what you need, we'll provide a clear project quote before any work begins. We don't publish a starting price, because a number without context tells you nothing useful.

    Can you redesign an existing website?

    Yes. Redesigns are a common starting point. We look at what's working, what isn't, and what's worth keeping, then rebuild it on a modern foundation. Existing content, search rankings, and URLs are handled carefully rather than thrown out.

    Can you build something custom?

    Yes. Websites are our primary service today, but we also build custom interfaces, booking and portal flows, and internal tools. If you're not sure whether your idea fits, describe it and we'll give you an honest answer, including when we're not the right team for it.

    Do you work with businesses outside the US?

    Our focus is the United States, but we work remotely and take on projects elsewhere when they're a good fit. Everything happens over email and video calls, so location has never been the deciding factor.

    Do you provide ongoing support?

    Yes. After launch you can keep us on for updates, changes, and improvements. We agree on what that looks like as part of the project. It's optional, and never billed automatically.

    Who owns the website when it's finished?

    You do. The design, the content, the code, and the domain belong to your business. Nothing is locked to a platform you have to keep paying us for.
