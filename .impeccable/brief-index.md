# Surface brief — index.html (home, single page)

Scope: the whole one-page marketing site. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience and job: a US small-business owner (restaurant, barber, dental, auto) reading on a phone
between customers, deciding within a minute whether VINCERE could make their business look
significantly more professional online. Action: Start a project (form or email). Secondary: View our work.

Proof and content: approved copy for every section (unchanged in this restyle); four self-initiated
concept interfaces labeled as concepts; the Ascendente wordmark (mono). No client logos, ratings,
counts, testimonials, team, or address exist and none may be implied.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; Google Fonts only; AA contrast; reduced motion;
keep every section, its order, and its copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The bright agency page, chosen on purpose as the category standard and executed at the craft
level of Smultron (warmth, composition), Linear and Stripe (precision). What it refuses: the austere
editorial minimalism of the previous build, and every trust device the category leans on that VINCERE
cannot truthfully show — the proof is the concept interfaces themselves, shown big and real.

OWN-WORLD: White sheet with a cool tint band (#F5F7FB) for alternating sections; navy ink #0B1F3A;
one gradient, mint→azure (#19B394→#2D7CF6), worn by a single keyword in the hero, the hero's
device stage, numerals, icons and hover states; one vermilion→raspberry gradient (#D93A1F→#C8206A) worn only by the primary pill "Start a project" — deepened from the reference's #FF5A3C so white 15px/600 label text clears 4.5:1 (AA) instead of ~3:1. Type: Onest (700 headings, 400/500 body, 300 for
ledes ≥18px); wordmark stays Archivo. Radius: 8px cards (services, why-free list, work, FAQ), 16px stage, form, detail and about panels, 9999px pills. Soft
offset shadows only. Tilted device mockups as the signature material. Removing the content, you
would still recognize: the curved-edge gradient stage, coral pill, navy ink, rounded cards.

STORY: The visitor sees, within one viewport, a modern company that builds modern interfaces —
demonstrated by real concept sites tilted on a gradient stage — understands that websites are the
first service and software the direction, believes the work is careful because the page is, and
starts a project through the one coral action that repeats at the end.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky white nav: wordmark left, four links, coral pill right. Left column (52%):
H1 in Onest 700 with "digital presence" in the mint→azure gradient; lede in 300; coral pill
"Start a project →" plus text link "View our work"; three check items. Right column: a gradient stage
bleeding to the right viewport edge with a large curved left edge, holding three tilted device
elevations of the concept sites (Sasso phone front at −9°, the focal device; Northline browser card behind at −6°; Apex phone right at −11°, its edge carrying the bleed),
overlapping, soft shadows, with a visible caption naming them as concept interfaces. Primary action sits at the left column's base line.

FORM: The canon (category standard) taken by the user as the standing exit from the direction round;
my grounded list ranked it outside the seven (it was the rut); seed key d29e6850, kind canon.
Signature interaction: the hero devices drift slowly on a shared clock and the stage's gradient
angle follows the pointer by a few degrees; sections reveal once on scroll with a 12px rise; the
coral pill has real press travel. Motion grammar: ease-out expo, 400–700 ms, one clock.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
